This is the operative K.I.N.D Privacy Policy carried into the Milla & Vida design, including the full sub-processor list and the real hosting locations.

1. Who we are

Milla & Vida is operated by K.I.N.D Technologies Ltd, registered in England and Wales (company number 17260532), registered office 33 Townsend Road, Stratford-upon-Avon, CV37 7DE, United Kingdom.

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, K.I.N.D Technologies Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through our website and platform. For the business contacts we find and write to on a client's behalf, we act as a data processor under the client's instructions, as governed by our Data Processing Agreement.

We are a UK company and the people we contact for clients are currently in the United States. For California residents we honour CCPA rights as set out in section 6.

2. Data we collect

Account data

Name, email address, company name, country and billing details. Collected at onboarding. Used to deliver the service and process payments.

Prospect data

B2B prospect names, work email addresses, job titles, company names, LinkedIn URLs and country, plus the public business facts that give a dated reason to get in touch (such as a new role, a funding round, an expansion or a new tool). Sourced on clients' behalf from Apollo.io, a licensed B2B data provider, and from public sources such as company announcements, news, job postings and role changes. Processed only to provide a client's service and under our Data Processing Agreement.

Usage data

Platform activity, API call logs and feature usage patterns. Used to deliver and improve the service and for billing accuracy.

Communication data

Email content sent on a client's behalf, replies received and opt-out requests. Stored so that replies can be answered the same day, opt-outs are honoured, and we keep a record of what was sent to each person.

Technical data

IP address, browser type, device type and referring URL. Collected automatically via server logs. Retained for 90 days.

3. How we use your data

  • Deliver and operate the Milla client experience and the Vida execution environment
  • Process payments via Stripe
  • Send service communications: alerts, meeting notices, reports and billing receipts. We do not send you marketing emails unless you have asked for them
  • Help our team research and draft using Anthropic’s Claude API. We send Anthropic only what each task needs, such as a prospect’s name, role, company and the text of their reply when drafting an answer. A person checks every message before it is sent
  • Comply with legal obligations under UK GDPR, the CAN-SPAM Act and other applicable data laws
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents
  • Improve the platform using anonymised, aggregated usage data

Lawful basis (UK GDPR): contract performance (delivering the service you signed up for); legitimate interests (contacting business people about something relevant to their work, for a reason we can state; security, fraud prevention, platform improvement); legal obligation (compliance, tax records). We do not rely on consent to contact prospects.

Google Calendar

If you connect your Google Calendar, Milla & Vida uses it only to book the meetings your prospects choose: we read your free/busy times and your calendar's time zone, create the meeting your prospect picks in your calendar, and read your Google account's email address so you are invited to it. We do not read the titles, descriptions, attendees or locations of your other events, and we do not change or delete them. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and is not shared with anyone except to provide this booking feature. You can disconnect at any time in Milla → Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Milla & Vida's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data hosting and transfers

Database

All client account data and prospect data is stored on Supabase in the eu-west-1 region (Dublin, Ireland). Our database provider is SOC 2 Type II audited.

Application hosting

The platform runs on Railway in its US West region, which operates on SOC 2-audited infrastructure. No personal data is stored in environment variables — only configuration keys.

International clients

Client data is stored in the European Union (Dublin, eu-west-1) and processed by application servers in the United States (Railway, US West). Where data crosses borders we apply appropriate safeguards.

Email delivery

Emails to prospects are sent in the client's name, from the client's own mailboxes (Google Workspace where we set them up). Resend delivers our service emails and receives replies.

AI processing

Research help, drafting of individual emails and reply drafting use Anthropic’s Claude API (US-based). A person checks every message before it is sent. To do this we send Anthropic the business-contact details each task needs, such as a prospect’s name, job title, company and the text of their reply, and nothing beyond that. Anthropic processes this data as our sub-processor.

Payments

Payment card data is processed by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 certified). We never store card numbers.

5. Data retention

  • Account data — held for the duration of your account and for 90 days after it closes
  • Prospect personal data — deleted 90 days after it was last used for a client, and in any case held for no longer than 90 days after the client's account closes, then permanently deleted (or sooner on request)
  • Email content — held for the duration of your account and for 90 days after it closes, then permanently deleted (or sooner on request)
  • Billing records — 7 years (UK tax law requirement)
  • Server logs — 90 days automatic rotation
  • Opt-out records — permanently retained, in order to honour the opt-out

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by emailing privacy@get-kind.com. We action deletion within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR (wherever you live)

  • Right of access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion
  • Right to portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint

If we have emailed you (anyone, anywhere)

  • Right to stop all further email from us, on any client's behalf, with one click or one reply, honoured permanently
  • Right to ask where we got your details and why we contacted you
  • Right to complain to us at privacy@get-kind.com, and to the data-protection regulator where you live

Under CCPA (California residents)

  • Right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell personal information

To exercise any right, email privacy@get-kind.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Prospect data and opt-outs

Prospect data is sourced from Apollo.io, a licensed B2B data provider, and from public sources such as company announcements, news, job postings and role changes. We use it only to decide whether a person has a real reason to hear from a client now, and to write to them individually. We process it on behalf of clients as a data processor under our Data Processing Agreement.

Every email we send for a client includes a clear way to opt out. Vida campaigns carry it on every send. An opt-out is written to our suppression list the moment it arrives, is logged with an audit trail, and is honoured permanently.

If you have received an email from us on a client's behalf and wish to be removed from all future emails, email privacy@get-kind.com with "Unsubscribe" in the subject line. To ask why we contacted you, write to the same address.

8. Third-party sub-processors

We use the following sub-processors to deliver the service. Each has been assessed for UK GDPR purposes and a Data Processing Agreement is in place where required.

  • Supabase — database hosting, Dublin, eu-west-1, European Union. SOC 2 Type II
  • Railway — application hosting, US West, United States. SOC 2-audited infrastructure
  • Stripe — payment processing, United States. PCI DSS Level 1 certified
  • Resend — service emails and receiving replies, United States
  • Google Workspace — business mailboxes we set up for a client's emails, United States. Where a client sends from mailboxes it already owns, that provider is the client's own supplier
  • Apollo.io — B2B contact data, United States. Our only source of prospect contact details (names, job titles and work email addresses); the dated reasons we get in touch come from public sources such as company announcements, news, job postings and role changes
  • Anthropic — AI language model, United States. Receives the business-contact details each task needs (see section 4)

The full sub-processor list is also documented in our Data Processing Agreement.

9. Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies. Session cookies are required for login and expire when you close your browser or after 30 days of inactivity. Preference cookies remember settings such as theme and expire after 12 months.

We run no website analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no advertising cookie and no visitor-tracking script of any kind on this site. Google Analytics was present on this website until 20 August 2026 and has been removed.

Embedded third parties, in full. No page on this site carries an embed from another company. Our product videos are served from our own website and set no cookie. Supabase — our own platform backend, not a third-party tracker.

Our emails do not track you. We do not place an invisible tracking pixel in outreach email, so we do not record whether you opened a message. This changed on 20 August 2026; before that date our sequence emails did contain an open-tracking pixel.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings — this may affect platform functionality. See our Cookie Policy for the same detail in one place.
