We find real people who match that targeting, show you a free Proof sample before you pay anything, prepare the outreach, and — once you have approved the exact package — send it on your behalf and work towards booked meetings with your prospects.

4. Programmes & Billing

Your Programme

You choose the number of booked meetings you want your programme to aim for. The price of your programme is shown to you before you commit, and it is the price you pay. There is no subscription.

Two Payments

  • Payment 1 — half of the programme price — authorises us to source and prepare your programme. Nothing is sent to anybody on the strength of Payment 1.
  • Payment 2 — the other half — is taken when you approve the prepared package, and it authorises outreach.

Pausing Before Go-Live

You may pause your programme at any time. If you pause before your programme goes live, Payment 2 is never charged.

The Meeting Number Is a Target

The number of booked meetings you choose is a target we work towards, not a guarantee. We work your programme up to a limit we set, and then we stop.

If We Deliver Fewer Meetings

If your programme ends having delivered fewer booked meetings than your target, the difference — at the price per meeting you bought at — is credited to your K.I.N.D account. Account credit can be used towards the first payment of a future programme; part of every payment is always made by card. Account credit is not paid back to your card, and it does not expire.

No Refunds

Programme payments are non-refundable. Where value is owed to you for meetings not delivered, it is credited to your account as described above.

Taxes

All prices are exclusive of applicable taxes. You are responsible for any taxes applicable in your jurisdiction.

Failed Payments

If a programme payment fails, the stage that payment would authorise does not start until it is completed.

8. Data, Privacy & Compliance

Data We Process

K.I.N.D processes personal data of your prospects (name, email, job title, company, LinkedIn URL) sourced from Apollo.io's database of 250M+ verified B2B contacts. This data is used solely to run outreach campaigns on your behalf.

Consent & Opt-Out

Every outreach email sent by K.I.N.D includes an unsubscribe link. Any prospect who clicks the unsubscribe link, or replies requesting removal, is immediately added to a permanent opt-out blocklist. Opted-out contacts are never contacted again by any K.I.N.D client.

POPIA (South Africa)

K.I.N.D operates in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). The 3-sequence outreach model constitutes a legitimate interest basis for initial contact. Opt-outs are honoured immediately and permanently.

GDPR (European Union & UK)

For clients targeting EU or UK-based prospects, outreach is conducted on a legitimate interest basis under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Opt-outs are treated as withdrawal of consent and actioned immediately.

CAN-SPAM (United States)

All emails include a physical address, clear identification of the sender, and a functioning opt-out mechanism in compliance with the CAN-SPAM Act.

Data Retention

Prospect data is retained for the duration of your active account and for 90 days following cancellation. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting hello@get-kind.com.

Sub-processors

K.I.N.D uses the following sub-processors: Apollo.io (lead data), Supabase (database — Dublin, eu-west-1), Resend (email delivery), Anthropic (AI email writing), Stripe (payment processing), Railway (application hosting). The current sub-processor list is also maintained in our Data Processing Agreement.
