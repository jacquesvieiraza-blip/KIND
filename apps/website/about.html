About M&V

We built the tool
we wished we had.

Great salespeople spend half their week hunting for people to sell to. Not selling — hunting. We built Milla and Vida so that work is simply done: you say what you want to achieve, and the meetings arrive.

The story

A sales rep who got
tired of the noise.

Before building M&V, our founder was an Account Executive. The job description said “close deals.” The reality was spending more time building prospect lists than actually selling — scrubbing spreadsheets, cold-calling directories, chasing leads that had nothing to do with what the company actually sold.

The problem wasn’t effort. The problem was signal. Pipeline quality was buried under volume. The whole model assumed that if you spray enough emails, something sticks. It does — but at a cost. Wasted hours. Burned relationships. Reps demoralised before they pick up the phone.

So the question became: what if the filtering happened before the outreach? What if every lead you spoke to was already a qualified fit — right industry, right title, right company size, right moment?

That question became a tool. The tool became M&V.

We didn’t build M&V to replace salespeople. The conversation, the relationship, the close — that’s irreplaceable. We built M&V to give reps their time back — to do the one thing AI still can’t: show up, listen, and earn trust.
