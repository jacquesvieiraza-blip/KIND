Drop 03: Payments & Billing

K.I.N.D Team June 2026 3 min read

Software billing is usually a black box. Drop 03 is Payments & Billing built the opposite way — transparent, usage-based, and impossible to drift out of sync.

You pay for outcomes, and you can see exactly what every cent bought.

What shipped in Drop 03

Stripe Checkout

Secure checkout with idempotent webhooks — payments that never double-charge, even if the network hiccups.

Credit Bundles

Buy lead-gen and FIGSY credits in bundles and top up only when you need to — no forced subscription you forget to cancel.

Agent Subscriptions

Add Vida, Milla or Denise as monthly agents and scale the family to fit your business as it grows.

Atomic Credit Ledger

Every credit is tracked in a tamper-proof ledger, so your balance and history always reconcile to the cent.

Usage Tracking

See exactly what each credit bought — every send, every reply, per client — full visibility, no surprises.

Pay for outcomes, not seats

Usage-based billing means your spend tracks the value you get — not a flat fee whether you use it or not.

The bottom line

Drop 03 makes the money side honest: secure payments, transparent credits, a ledger that always reconciles, and a clear record of what you got for what you paid.
