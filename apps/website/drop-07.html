Drop 07 · RevOps

Your data is lying to youLive

Most pipelines run on numbers nobody actually checks — stale stages, “qualified” leads that aren’t, forecasts built on hope. The dashboard looks confident; the data underneath isn’t.

See how FIGSY works →
From the problem to the fix
The pain

You decide on these numbers

You make real decisions — hiring, spend, targets — on those numbers. When they’re wrong, you don’t find out at the dashboard. You find out at the end of the quarter, when the pipeline that looked healthy doesn’t close.

The metric

Data hygiene

The number is the share of your pipeline that’s actually current and verified. In a lot of CRMs it’s shockingly low, and nobody measures it, so the rot stays invisible until it costs you.

The cost of inaction

Worse than no data

Decisions made on bad data are worse than decisions made on none — because you trust them. You’ll double down on a channel that isn’t working and starve one that is, and the numbers will keep telling you it’s fine.

How K.I.N.D solves it

Numbers that are actually true

The fix isn’t a better dashboard — it’s data that maintains itself. K.I.N.D keeps the underlying data honest: leads enriched from live sources, status driven by actual replies and bookings, not manual guesswork. The numbers you decide on are the numbers that are true.
