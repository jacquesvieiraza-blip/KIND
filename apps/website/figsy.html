How the engine works
Source. Write. Follow up. Book.

FIGSY runs the full outbound loop under Milla&Vida — from finding your ideal customer to booking the meeting. Our team drives it; you approve the leads it surfaces in your Milla portal.

Step 01

ICP-matched lead sourcing

Searches targeted, verified B2B contacts. Scores every match 0–100 against your Ideal Customer Profile. Only decision-makers above the threshold get through.

Step 02

Hyper-personalised outreach

Claude writes a unique email for every lead — role, company context, industry signals. No templates. No spray-and-pray. Every message sounds like a human who did their homework.

Step 03

Reply tracking & classification

FIGSY reads and classifies every reply — positive intent, objection, or unsubscribe — and surfaces it for our operator team to action, with the warm ones flagged hot to you. Unsubscribes are suppressed permanently with consent logged.

Booking link in every email

Every email FIGSY sends carries your booking link, so a ready prospect grabs a time that suits them — straight into your calendar. You get the notification. You show up. That’s the only part you ever touch.
