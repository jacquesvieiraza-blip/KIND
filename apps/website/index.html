Questions people actually ask.

Will FIGSY send emails without my approval?+

Outbound sequences run automatically, but every reply FIGSY drafts waits for your approval before it sends. You stay in control of the conversation — FIGSY just does the heavy lifting.

What counts as a "qualified lead"?+

A verified decision-maker who scores above your threshold against your Ideal Customer Profile (0–100). You only pay the $1 reveal when a lead clears that bar — never for raw contacts.

Will this damage my domain reputation?+

FIGSY paces sending, personalises every message, and auto-pauses any campaign that drops below a 1% reply rate — so no credits are burned and your domain stays healthy.

Is there really no subscription?+

Really. $1 to reveal a qualified lead, $3 for FIGSY to fully work it — $4 total. No setup fees, no seats, no monthly minimum. Stop any time.
