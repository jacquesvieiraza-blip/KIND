1. Appointment

K.I.N.D (Registered in England & Wales) appoints you as a non-exclusive partner to refer, resell, or integrate the K.I.N.D platform under the tier you have applied for. This agreement does not create employment, agency (legal), or joint venture.

2. Commission & Payment

  • Referral tier: 20% of monthly subscription revenue per referred client for 12 months.
  • Agency tier: 25% recurring commission for the lifetime of the referred client relationship.
  • White-label tier: 30% recurring commission for the lifetime of the relationship.

Commissions are calculated monthly and paid within 30 days of month-end via Wise transfer. Minimum payout threshold: R500 / $30. Commissions are not payable on refunded or charged-back amounts.

3. Deal Registration & Protection

You may register a prospect through the partner portal before making contact. Registered deals receive 60-day exclusive protection — K.I.N.D will not directly solicit that prospect during the protection window. Protection lapses if the deal is not progressed within 60 days. K.I.N.D reserves the right to reject registrations that are inaccurate or registered in bad faith.

4. Partner Obligations

  • Represent K.I.N.D accurately and in compliance with provided marketing materials.
  • Obtain all necessary consents before sharing prospect data with K.I.N.D (POPIA / GDPR compliance).
  • Not make commitments or warranties on behalf of K.I.N.D beyond stated product capabilities.
  • Promptly notify K.I.N.D of any complaints, disputes, or material issues with referred clients.

5. Intellectual Property

K.I.N.D grants you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the K.I.N.D brand, logo, and marketing materials solely for authorised partner activities. All IP remains the property of K.I.N.D. You may not modify, sub-licence, or use the brand outside of approved partner materials.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business information, pricing structures, client data, and technical specifications shared in connection with this agreement. This obligation survives termination for a period of 2 years.

7. Term & Termination

This agreement begins on the date of approval and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. K.I.N.D may terminate immediately for material breach, fraud, or reputational harm. Commissions already earned and approved at the time of termination will be paid out within 60 days.

8. Liability

K.I.N.D's total liability under this agreement is limited to commissions paid to you in the 3 months preceding any claim. K.I.N.D is not liable for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.

9. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England & Wales. Disputes shall be resolved by the courts of England & Wales. For South African partners, the agreement is additionally subject to compliance with South African law where applicable.
