THE OUTCOME

The outcome you pay for: a Qualified Meeting.

A meeting counts toward your target only when all seven of these are met and it is booked.

01

The prospect falls within your approved ICP and targeting criteria.

02

The individual meets the agreed role, seniority or buying-influence criteria.

03

The prospect has positively agreed to a meeting with you.

04

The meeting has been scheduled for an agreed date and time.

05

The prospect has demonstrated genuine relevance to your stated offer, problem or service area.

06

The prospect is not an existing customer, active opportunity or excluded account, where that information has been made available to us.

07

We can evidence the prospect’s acceptance of the meeting.

Not guaranteed

That the prospect will attend, will purchase or has an approved budget; that the opportunity will progress; the value of any resulting opportunity; the performance of your salespeople; or any particular conversion or revenue outcome.

No-shows and challenges

If a prospect doesn’t attend or cancels, we reschedule once at no extra charge. A challenge to whether a meeting was qualified is raised within 3 business days of it being booked. The full rules are in our Terms.
