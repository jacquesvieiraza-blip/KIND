Company — Our Story

We built the SDR we never could afford to hire.

K.I.N.D exists because the outbound stack was broken. Too many tools, too much manual work, too few results — and no affordable solution for teams without a full sales ops department behind them.

Where it started

K.I.N.D was built by a sales operator who had spent years running outbound campaigns by hand. Every morning started the same way: a spreadsheet of 200 leads, a blank email client, and the knowledge that today’s pipeline was entirely dependent on today’s manual effort.

The tools existed, separately. Apollo for leads. Salesloft for sequences. A copywriter to personalise at scale. Someone to manage the replies. Someone else to chase the follow-ups. Book the meetings. Log the calls. For a growth-stage team, that was a $180,000+ annual spend before a single deal closed.

The problem wasn’t access to leads. The problem was that every piece of the outbound machine required a human to keep it moving. The moment you stopped pushing, the pipeline stopped flowing.

“What if the whole thing ran while you slept? Not scheduled emails — but genuine, autonomous outreach that read replies, handled objections, and booked the meeting.”

Why we built it for Africa

Most outbound platforms are built for US and European sales motions. Linear funnels. Cold email only. No WhatsApp. Compliance frameworks that don’t map to POPIA. Pricing models that assume a 100-person sales team and a full-time ops resource to run the tool.

African B2B sales is different. WhatsApp is the primary first-contact channel. Decision-makers are accessible but relationship-driven. The sales cycle requires consistent presence, not high-volume spray. And compliance, particularly POPIA, is not optional — it’s a legal obligation.

K.I.N.D was designed for this reality from day one. Not adapted to it later. The platform is global-first in architecture, compliance, and philosophy.

What we’re building toward

The goal is not to build a better cold email tool. The goal is to make an autonomous AI sales team accessible to every B2B business on the continent — regardless of whether they have a sales ops budget, an SDR team, or a growth consultant on retainer.

FIGSY opens the door and books the meetings. Milla reads the signals and tells you what to do next. Vida converts inbound at the front door. Denise closes. Four agents that collapse what used to cost $200,000 a year into a single platform that runs continuously, autonomously, and compliantly.

We’re at the beginning of that story. And the teams building pipeline on K.I.N.D today are the proof it works.

The names behind the names

Milla, Vida and Denise aren’t product names invented in a boardroom. They’re the founder’s family — his two daughters and his mother — and their personalities are the product.

The Brain

Milla

My eldest daughter. Razor-sharp — she’ll catch the flaw in your logic before you’ve finished the sentence, and she’s usually three steps ahead. The critical thinker. That’s the Milla agent: reading your business, questioning the obvious, and surfacing intelligence before you know you need it.

The Connector

Vida

My youngest daughter. Always talking, always smiling cheek to cheek — she lights up every room and makes everyone feel like they belong. The chatterbox who connects. Vida the agent has that same energy: on your website, on WhatsApp, 24/7 — never missing a conversation, never letting a warm lead go cold.

The Closer

Denise

My mother. She built a successful sales business from nothing — and closed not by pushing, but because people genuinely liked and trusted her. The relationship closer. That’s the Denise agent: warm, confident, zero desperation — she picks up where FIGSY leaves off and turns a booked meeting into a signed deal.

How we got here

2023

The problem becomes undeniable

A sales operator running outbound manually hits a wall. Apollo, Salesloft, a copywriter, and a VA: $180k/year to fill a calendar. The system is too fragile, too expensive, and too slow. The question becomes: what would an autonomous version of this look like?

2024

First version of FIGSY

An autonomous SDR built on Claude. Finds leads, writes personalised emails, handles replies, books meetings. The first campaigns go live. Reply rates outperform manual outreach. The case for full automation is proven.

2025

Four agents: FIGSY, Milla, Vida & Denise

The platform expands. Milla adds the intelligence layer — campaign analytics, ICP refinement, the Monday digest. Vida adds inbound qualification on web and WhatsApp. K.I.N.D becomes a complete autonomous revenue system.

2026

Platform opens across Africa

K.I.N.D opens to B2B teams across South Africa, East Africa, and West Africa. POPIA-compliant by default. WhatsApp-native. First campaigns live in 5 business days. Unlimited pipeline from day one.
