FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Book a walkthrough and we set everything up for you — your ideal customer profile, your campaign, the lot. Leads then land in your Milla portal for you to review and approve. Free Proof comes first; the programme is priced per booked meeting.

An ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) defines who you want to reach — industry, job title, company size, geography, and seniority level. You tell us once (in your walkthrough or by chatting to your operator) and our team builds and runs it for you. Matched, scored leads then land masked in your Milla portal for you to review and approve.

Each lead receives an AI score from 0–100 based on how closely they match your ICP. Factors include: job title match, seniority match, company size fit, industry alignment, and geographic relevance. Leads scoring 70+ are considered high-fit. You can filter your leads pipeline by score to prioritise your outreach.

No. You only pay for leads that are genuinely new to your pipeline. M&V deduplicates against your existing leads — if a contact has already been delivered to you, they won’t appear again and you won’t be charged. We also check against your CRM when you connect it. If a lead is already in your CRM, they won’t be delivered.

Privacy, suppression and jurisdiction-aware controls are built into the product, not added on. We contact people on a legitimate-interest basis. Every message carries a one-click unsubscribe and our postal address. Opt-out requests are applied immediately, permanently, and shared across all M&V clients (so a contact who opts out of one client’s outreach is protected across all clients). Data is stored in the European Union (Dublin, eu-west-1) on encrypted, SOC 2 Type II-audited infrastructure, and processed by application servers in the United States (Railway, US West).

Vida is the engine under Milla&Vida. When you approve the programme, our team uses Vida to write a unique personalised email (not a template — personalised to the lead’s role, company, and industry), send it, follow up, and drop a booking link in every email so the lead can grab a time. Replies are triaged with human oversight. You just show up to the booked calls. The model is simple: $450 for a booked meeting in your calendar (one flat price) — Free Proof first, no subscriptions.

There’s nothing to cancel — M&V has no subscription. You buy a programme, we deliver it, and you decide whether to buy another. Pause before go-live and the second half is never charged. No lock-in, no annual contract, no minimums.

All charges are in USD. A programme is paid in two halves — half to start the build, half before it goes live — with no subscription, no annual contract and no lock-in. Contact hello@get-kind.com if you have any billing questions.

Check your spam/junk folder first. If it’s not there, email hello@get-kind.com with the email address you signed up with and we’ll resend the confirmation or manually verify your account.

Connecting a CRM means HubSpot or Pipedrive — those are the two we integrate with. Connect one in Settings and we de-duplicate against it, so you’re never charged for a contact you already have. We do not integrate with Salesforce or Zoho. If you use one of those — or any other CRM — email hello@get-kind.com and we’ll send your programme leads as a CSV you can import anywhere; there is no self-serve export button yet.
