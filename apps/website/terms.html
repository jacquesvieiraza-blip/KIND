2. Services

The Managed Programme (powered by Vida, the engine)

A managed outbound programme priced on booked meetings. You choose a target number of booked meetings and give us your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). Following the first payment, our team sources and qualifies the audience, prepares the outreach sequence and the sending identity, and presents the complete programme to you in Milla. You approve that programme as presented. Following the second payment and your instruction to go live, we run the outreach and follow-up on your behalf, triage replies with human oversight, and book meetings into your calendar.

4. Programme Pricing & Payment

Programme pricing

K.I.N.D operates on a programme model, in US dollars. A programme is priced on the number of booked meetings you choose. The price per booked meeting is $450 for a single meeting and reduces as the programme size increases, on the published pricing curve, to a floor of $400 per booked meeting. Your programme price is the number of meetings multiplied by the applicable rate, and is shown to you in full before you commit.

There is no subscription, no retainer, no per-seat licence, and no wallet, credits or top-ups. This is not a wallet top-up model and no balance is held on your account.

Getting started — the first payment

Your first purchase is the first payment: 50% of your programme price. It authorises us to source the audience, qualify it, and prepare the outreach sequence and your sending identity up to the programme's recommended volume. Preparation begins once it lands.

The second payment

The remaining 50% of your programme price falls due after you have approved the prepared programme and before it goes live. No outreach is sent until it is paid and you have instructed us to go live.

Pausing

You may pause at any point between the two payments. If you pause before go-live, the second payment is never taken. Any programme value you have paid for and not used remains on your account and does not expire.

Refunds

The first payment is final and is not refundable once preparation has begun — it pays for the sourcing, qualification and preparation work produced for your programme, whether or not you subsequently approve it. The second payment is not taken unless you have approved the programme and instructed us to go live.

The meeting target is a target and not a guarantee, and neither payment is contingent on the number of meetings produced.

Taxes

All prices are exclusive of applicable taxes. You are responsible for any taxes applicable in your jurisdiction.

Suspension for Non-Payment

If a payment due under your programme is not completed within 7 days of falling due, K.I.N.D reserves the right to suspend outreach services. Account data is retained for 90 days following suspension.

5. What Constitutes a Qualified Prospect and a Booked Meeting

A qualified prospect is a net-new contact matching your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), with a verified, contactable email address, with opt-out and consent screening applied, and not already present in your connected CRM. Qualified prospects form the audience of the programme presented to you for approval.

A booked meeting is a confirmed slot accepted in your calendar by a real person at a company that matches the brief. A reply on its own is not a booked meeting.

Reviewing is always free: you are never charged for looking at Free Proof, at the prepared programme, or at any prospect within it.

A programme is paid for in the two instalments described in Section 4, regardless of subsequent engagement. If prospects in your programme do not respond, reply negatively, or do not convert to a meeting, the programme price is not adjusted retrospectively.

K.I.N.D makes no guarantee regarding conversion rates, meeting bookings, or sales outcomes. Results vary based on ICP quality, industry, geography, and market conditions.

8. Data, Privacy & Compliance

Data We Process

K.I.N.D processes personal data of your prospects (name, email, job title, company) sourced from licensed B2B data providers (PeopleDataLabs, Hunter and — where you configure it — Apollo.io). This data is used solely to run outreach campaigns on your behalf.

Consent & Opt-Out

Every outreach email sent by K.I.N.D includes an unsubscribe link. Any prospect who clicks the unsubscribe link, or replies requesting removal, is immediately added to a permanent opt-out blocklist. Opted-out contacts are never contacted again by any K.I.N.D client.

POPIA (South Africa)

K.I.N.D operates in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). The outreach sequence model (up to 7 steps) constitutes a legitimate interest basis for initial contact. Opt-outs are honoured immediately and permanently.

GDPR (European Union & UK)

For clients targeting EU or UK-based prospects, outreach is conducted on a legitimate interest basis under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Opt-outs are treated as withdrawal of consent and actioned immediately.

CAN-SPAM (United States)

All emails include a physical address, clear identification of the sender, and a functioning opt-out mechanism in compliance with the CAN-SPAM Act.

Data Retention

Prospect data is retained while your account is active and for 90 days following account closure. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting privacy@get-kind.com.

Sub-processors

K.I.N.D uses the following sub-processors: PeopleDataLabs, Hunter and Apollo.io (lead data), Supabase (database — Dublin, eu-west-1), Resend (email delivery), Anthropic (AI email writing), Stripe (payment processing), Railway (application hosting). The current sub-processor list is also maintained in our Data Processing Agreement.
