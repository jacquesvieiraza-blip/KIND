A managed outbound programme priced on booked meetings. You choose a target number of booked meetings and give us your Ideal Customer Profile. 4. Programme pricing and payment

Programme pricing

We operate on a programme model, in US dollars. A programme is priced on the number of booked meetings you choose. The price per booked meeting is $450 for a single meeting and reduces as the programme size increases, on the published pricing curve, to a floor of $400 per booked meeting. Your programme price is the number of meetings multiplied by the applicable rate, and is shown to you in full before you commit. There is no subscription, no retainer and no per-seat licence, and no wallet, credits or top-ups — this is not a wallet top-up model and no balance is held on your account.

Getting started

Your first purchase is the first payment — 50% of your programme price. It authorises us to source the audience, qualify it, and prepare the outreach sequence and your sending identity up to the programme’s recommended volume. Preparation begins once it lands.

Second payment

The remaining 50% of your programme price falls due after you have approved the prepared programme and before it goes live. No outreach is sent until it is paid and you have instructed us to go live.

Pausing

You may pause at any point between the two payments. If you pause before go-live, the second payment is never taken. Any programme value you have paid for and not used remains on your account and does not expire.

Refunds

The first payment is final and is not refundable once preparation has begun — it pays for the sourcing, qualification and preparation work produced for your programme, whether or not you subsequently approve it. The second payment is not taken unless you have approved the programme and instructed us to go live. The meeting target is a target and not a guarantee, and neither payment is contingent on the number of meetings produced.

Taxes

All prices are exclusive of applicable taxes. You are responsible for any taxes applicable in your jurisdiction.

Suspension for non-payment

If a payment due under your programme is not completed within 7 days of falling due, we reserve the right to suspend outreach services. Account data is retained for 90 days following suspension.

5. What constitutes a qualified prospect and a booked meeting

A booked meeting is a confirmed slot accepted in your calendar by a real person at a company that matches the brief. A reply on its own is not a booked meeting.

A qualified prospect is a net-new contact matching your Ideal Customer Profile, with a verified, contactable email address, with opt-out and consent screening applied, and not already present in your connected CRM. Qualified prospects form the audience of the programme presented to you for approval.

A programme is paid for in the two instalments described above, regardless of subsequent engagement. If prospects in your programme do not respond, reply negatively, or do not convert to a meeting, the programme price is not adjusted retrospectively.

We make no guarantee regarding conversion rates, meeting bookings or sales outcomes. Results vary based on ICP quality, industry, geography and market conditions.

Consent and opt-out

Every outreach email includes an unsubscribe link. Any prospect who clicks the unsubscribe link, or replies requesting removal, is added to a permanent opt-out blocklist. Opted-out contacts are not contacted again.

POPIA (South Africa)

GDPR (European Union and UK)

For clients targeting EU or UK-based prospects, outreach is conducted on a legitimate interest basis under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Opt-outs are treated as withdrawal of consent and actioned immediately.

CAN-SPAM (United States)

Prospect data is retained while your account is active and for 90 days following account closure. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting privacy@get-kind.com.
