5. What counts as a held meeting

A held meeting is a meeting that meets all seven of the following conditions:

  1. The prospect falls within your approved ICP and targeting criteria.
  2. The individual meets the agreed role, seniority or buying-influence criteria.
  3. The prospect has positively agreed to a meeting with you.
  4. The meeting took place at the agreed date and time, with the agreed person.
  5. The prospect has demonstrated genuine relevance to your stated offer, problem or service area.
  6. The prospect is not an existing customer, active opportunity or excluded account, where that information has been made available to us.
  7. We can evidence the prospect’s acceptance of the meeting.

A reply on its own is not a held meeting, and neither is a meeting that is arranged but does not take place. You are charged $700 for a meeting only when all seven conditions are met.

The people we contact are business contacts at companies in your target market, each chosen because of a real, dated reason to talk to you now. Each has a verified work email address, is checked against our permanent opt-out list, and is not already in your connected CRM. You see who we plan to contact, and the message written to each of them, before anything is sent.

You pay for held meetings, not for effort. If people we contact do not reply, reply negatively or do not agree to meet, you are not charged for them. The setup fee is not adjusted for how many meetings follow.

We make no guarantee about reply rates, how many meetings are held or sales outcomes. We aim for a handful of good conversations a month, not a flood of maybes. Results vary with your market, your offer and timing.

Meetings that do not happen

If a meeting doesn't happen, you don't pay for it. A meeting that does not take place is not a held meeting and is not charged, except where you cancel it or do not attend (see below).

No-shows and cancellations

If a meeting does not take place, this is how it is treated:

  • The prospect does not attend: you are not charged, and we will make reasonable efforts to reschedule the meeting once.
  • The prospect cancels: you are not charged, and we will make reasonable efforts to reschedule the meeting once.
  • You cancel or do not attend: the meeting is charged as a held meeting unless you give us at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • The meeting is successfully rescheduled: it is charged once, when it is held, not twice.
  • The prospect cannot be rescheduled after reasonable attempts: you are not charged for that meeting.

Challenging a meeting

Any challenge to whether a meeting should be charged must be raised with us within 3 business days of the meeting taking place, and must identify which of the seven conditions above was not met.

A held meeting is still charged if, afterwards:

  • the prospect does not progress;
  • the prospect changes their mind;
  • the sales call goes badly;
  • you discover there is no immediate budget;
  • the opportunity is smaller than expected; or
  • the prospect does not ultimately purchase.

Qualification is judged against the information available at the point the meeting was booked, not against the eventual sales outcome.

Who is responsible for what

We are responsible for finding the companies with a real reason to talk to you now, researching each person, writing to each one individually, checking every message before it goes, answering replies the same day and arranging the meeting.

You are responsible for attending, conducting the sales conversation, discovery, proposal, negotiation and closing the business.

We are paid for the part we control, a meeting that takes place, and are not held financially responsible for the part of the sales process we do not control.
