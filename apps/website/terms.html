5. What Constitutes a Qualified Lead

A qualified lead is defined as a net-new prospect matching your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) that is delivered to your dashboard with a verified, contactable email address, with opt-out and consent screening applied, and that is not already present in your connected CRM. A credit is consumed when such a lead is delivered. Leads for which no contactable email address can be verified are not delivered and are not charged.

The following do not consume a credit:

  • No response to all 3 emails in the sequence
  • A negative reply ("not interested", "remove me", "no thank you", or similar)
  • An automated out-of-office reply
  • A click on the unsubscribe link

One credit is consumed per qualified lead, regardless of subsequent engagement. If a qualified lead later goes cold or does not convert to a meeting, the credit is not refunded.

K.I.N.D makes no guarantee regarding conversion rates, meeting bookings, or sales outcomes resulting from qualified leads. Results vary based on ICP quality, industry, geography, and market conditions.
