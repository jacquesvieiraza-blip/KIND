Financial Services

Compliant leads for
regulated markets.

Financial services firms operate in the most compliance-sensitive prospecting environment in any industry. GDPR in Europe, POPIA in South Africa, and FSCA requirements mean that how you source and contact prospects matters as much as who you contact.

K.I.N.D builds consent workflows and opt-out management directly into every lead — so your team can prospect with confidence, not legal anxiety.

  • GDPR & POPIA consent captured per lead before delivery
  • Opt-out blocklist — shared, instant, permanent
  • Target CFOs, Finance Directors, and Treasury teams by seniority
  • Industry filters: banking, insurance, asset management, fintech
  • Audit trail on every lead interaction for compliance reporting
Who we target

K.I.N.D helps financial services firms reach:

  • CFOs & Finance Directors — enterprise & mid-market
  • Treasury & investment managers
  • Business owners & directors of SMEs
  • Wealth management & retirement fund decision-makers
  • Procurement and operations leads at regulated entities

Find the hiring managers
before they post.

Recruitment agencies and staffing firms compete for the same mandates. The firms that win are the ones who reach hiring managers before the role is advertised — when the need is real but the brief isn't briefed yet.

K.I.N.D helps recruiters identify and reach HR Directors, People & Culture leads, and business owners at companies in active growth phases — before competitors are in the room.

  • Target CHRO, HR Director, Talent Acquisition, and People leads
  • Filter by headcount growth signals and job posting activity

  • FIGSY outreach that positions your firm before the brief lands
  • Full GDPR & POPIA compliance — critical for regulated recruitment markets
