Company — Values

The principles we build on.

Four values that guide every decision we make — about the product, the platform, and the people we build it for.

🔍
Transparency

No black boxes.

Every lead FIGSY sources, every email she sends, every reply she handles — fully visible, fully auditable. You always know exactly what your AI team is doing and why.

We believe that autonomy without visibility is just risk. Every decision the platform makes is logged, explainable, and reviewable. No opacity. No surprises.

🎯
Precision

Volume without precision is noise.

We score every lead. We contextualise every message. We monitor every campaign. More contacts does not mean more pipeline — better-targeted contacts does.

K.I.N.D is not built for spray-and-pray. It’s built for surgical outbound that earns replies because it deserves them.

🌍
global-first

Built on the continent. For the continent.

POPIA compliance is not a checkbox — it’s a default. WhatsApp is not an add-on — it’s core infrastructure. African B2B relationships are not an afterthought — they’re the entire design brief.

We did not take a US product and localise it. We built from the ground up for how African B2B sales actually works.

🤝
Customer obsession

We win when you win.

Our success metric is not accounts created or seats sold. It is meetings booked and pipeline built. If K.I.N.D is not generating real pipeline for you, we have failed — regardless of what the product does on paper.

This is why we guarantee your first campaign goes live in 5 business days. Not sometime. Not eventually. In 5 days.

Values in practice

What this looks like in the product

Full campaign audit trail

Every lead sourced, every email sent, every reply handled is logged with timestamp, reasoning, and outcome. No hidden actions.

ICP scoring, not volume

Leads are scored 0–100 against your ICP before a single email is sent. Only decision-makers above your threshold get through.

POPIA consent logging

Every suppression request is logged with timestamp and channel. Compliance is not a setting you turn on — it runs by default.

WhatsApp native

Vida runs on WhatsApp Business out of the box. No third-party integration fees. No API configuration required.

5-day launch guarantee

Your first campaign goes live within 5 business days of signing up, or we extend your trial. No exceptions.

Campaign auto-pause

If a campaign drops below 1% reply rate, FIGSY pauses it automatically and alerts you. No wasted credits. No babysitting required.
