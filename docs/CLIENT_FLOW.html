PART 2 · For a company — every rep their own FIGSY

augment the team, never replace it

The model (locked): we don't replace a sales org — we give every rep their own AI. Seats are free. The owner funds a company credit pool, each rep runs autonomously (own leads, own voice, own calendar), and the owner controls the budget centrally — paying only for the usage each rep consumes. Augment first; efficiency follows.
