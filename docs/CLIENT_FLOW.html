K.I.N.D — how the platform actually works

Lead Gen + Vida feed leads → FIGSY opens → Denise closes → Milla reads it all. Every outcome logged.

FIGSY — The Opener (AI SDR) Milla — The Brain (VA, $49/mo) Vida — The Connector (Chatbot, $29/mo) Denise — The Closer (AE, $99/mo) 
flowchart TD
    A([Client signs up]):::sys --> B[Onboarding: company, industry, country, website]:::sys
    B --> C[ICP auto-prefilled from the website]:::sys

    subgraph LG[LEAD GEN -- sourcing the right people]
      direction TB
      C --> D[Client builds / confirms ICP]
      D --> E[ICP run: Apollo sources matching contacts]
      E --> F[AI scores every lead 0-100 vs your ICP]
      F --> G{Score >= 60?}
      G -->|yes| H[Consent + auto-enrol into FIGSY]
      G -->|no| Z[Held back / refine ICP]
    end

    subgraph VID[VIDA -- The Connector]
      direction TB
      V1[One-line widget on your site + WhatsApp] --> V2[Qualifies every visitor 24/7]
      V2 --> V3[Captures inbound leads, routes hot ones to you]
    end
    V3 --> H

    subgraph FIG[FIGSY -- The Opener]
      direction TB
      H --> I[Campaign + multi-step email sequence]
      I --> J[[Cron every 2h: send due emails]]
      J --> K[AI writes each email in your voice -- sent via Resend]
      K --> L[Opens tracked - replies come back in]
      L --> M{Reply classified by AI}
      M -->|interested| N[Hot lead -> your Inbox]
      M -->|opt-out| O[Added to blocklist, sequence stops]
      N --> P[Prospect books via FIGSY booking link]
      P --> Q[[Calendar event created + meeting booked]]
      J -. auto-pause if reply rate < 1% .-> I
    end

    subgraph DEN[DENISE -- The Closer]
      direction TB
      Q --> R[Confirms the meeting -- warm, specific]
      R --> S[Drafts warm follow-ups to quiet prospects]
      S --> T[Turns your call notes into a proposal]
      T --> U[Deal closed]
    end

    subgraph MIL[MILLA -- The Brain]
      direction TB
      W1[Trained on your docs + whole pipeline] --> W2[Daily morning brief + anomaly alerts]
      W2 --> W3[Monday: weekly leads digest]
      W3 --> W4[Ask her anything: 'what's working?']
    end

    L -. signals .-> W1
    Q -. feeds results .-> W1
    Q --> DF[(Outcome events logged:
send - reply - opt_out - meeting_booked
