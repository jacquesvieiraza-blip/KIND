K.I.N.D · Per-Rep Model

One company. Every rep their own AI SDR.

An owner buys a FIGSY for each rep. Each rep gets their own autonomous sales rep — own leads, own voice, meetings booked onto their own calendar. The owner runs it all from one command centre.

The key difference from the old model: nothing is shared or centralised at the working level. Rep 1's FIGSY never touches Rep 2's leads, and a prospect that books with Rep 3 lands on Rep 3's calendar — not a shared company one. Ten reps = ten independent sales reps running in parallel.

Just like your company uses Claude: the company holds the budget; each rep works within their allocation; when someone needs more, they request it and the owner approves or denies. No per-seat fee — you pay only for usage, plus any agents (Milla $49 · Vida $29 · Denise $39) the owner switches on per rep. Centralised spend control, autonomous work per rep — revenue scales with usage. Outcome-based pricing comes next.
