## FOUNDER DECISIONS REQUIRED

### FD-01 — the benchmark
**~150 accepted/contacted prospects per booked meeting** (R69, 26 Aug) **vs 250 recommended leads per targeted booked meeting** (programme seed).

The denominators were tested, not assumed: R69 counts *accepted/contacted prospects*, the seed counts *recommended programme leads* — different stages, **except that the 1:1 commercial planning assumption collapses them onto the same denominator.** They are comparable and they disagree, and the seed places every client at R69's campaign-review threshold on day one.

**Money consequence:** at ~$450 and 70% contribution the COGS ceiling is ~$135; at 250 leads and 1:1, PDL alone is ~$70. **At 500 leads per meeting, contribution goes negative.**

🛑 **STATUS: UNRESOLVED — DO NOT SILENTLY RECONCILE.** — TR-015, TR-016

### FD-02 — the definition of "programme contribution"
The *direction* is settled: **contribution, not gross**. **What counts as contribution is not**: which costs are deducted, at what point, and whether it is measured per programme or per period. Until defined it cannot be built or quoted to a partner. Meanwhile the legacy path still derives `PARTNER_COMMISSION_PER_LEAD_USD` from `LEAD_PRICE_USD`, so **any per-lead price change silently rewrites partner earnings.**

🛑 **STATUS: UNRESOLVED.** — TR-011

### FD-03 — the portal UI acceptance bar
Portal UI quality is **launch-critical direction** and settled as such. **The decision required is what "good enough to launch" means** — the acceptance criteria a build can be measured against. Three UI docs exist (`DESIGN-REFERENCE.md`, `portal-v2-layout.md`, `onboarding-tour-buildplan.md`) and **none records a bar**, so no build can currently be judged complete.

🛑 **STATUS: UNRESOLVED — a decision, not an unknown.** — TR-051
