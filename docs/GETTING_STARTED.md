# Getting Started with K.I.N.D

Welcome. This is your AI revenue team — they find the right people, open the
conversation in your voice, book meetings onto your calendar, and help you close.
This guide gets you from signed-up to first outreach in about **10 minutes**.

You do the six steps below in order. Each takes 1–2 minutes.

---

## Meet your team

| Agent | Role | What they do |
|-------|------|--------------|
| **FIGSY** | The Opener (AI SDR) | Finds ideal clients, writes in your voice, follows up, books the meeting |
| **Milla** | The Brain | Reads your pipeline and tells you what's working and what to change |
| **Vida** | The Connector | Website + WhatsApp chatbot — qualifies every visitor 24/7 |
| **Denise** | The Closer (AI AE) | Confirms meetings, handles objections, drafts proposals |

You don't need to set up all four to get value. **Start with FIGSY** — that's
steps 2–5 below.

---

## Step 1 — Your business profile ✅ (already done)

When you signed up, FIGSY asked you a few questions — your company, what you do,
where you're based, and your website. That's your business profile, and it's
already saved. It powers every agent, so everything below is personalised to you.

*Want to edit it later?* **Settings → Business profile.**

---

## Step 2 — Confirm your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Why:** This is who FIGSY targets. Get it right and every lead is relevant.

We already **scanned your website and pre-filled a draft ICP** for you — target
industries, job titles, seniority, company size, and geography. Your job is just
to check it.

1. Go to **Leads → ICP**
2. Read through the draft. Adjust anything that's off.
3. Stuck? Hit **"Suggest with AI"** and we'll propose one from your profile.
4. Save.

> Tip: tighter is better. "Heads of Finance at SA logistics firms, 50–200 staff"
> beats "anyone in business." FIGSY does best with a clear target.

---

## Step 3 — Connect your sending email

**Why:** So FIGSY sends from **your** address — your name, your domain — not ours.
This is what makes the outreach feel personal and land in inboxes.

1. Go to **Settings → Email**
2. Connect your sending mailbox (follow the on-screen prompts)
3. Send yourself a test to confirm it works

> Deliverability matters. Use a real, warmed-up mailbox you already send from.
> If you're brand new to cold email, talk to us first — we'll help you warm up.

---

## Step 4 — Review your first leads

**Why:** Nothing goes out without your say-so. You stay in control.

Once your ICP is set, leads start arriving — **AI-sourced and scored 0–100** on
how well they fit.

1. Go to **Leads**
2. Skim the list. Higher score = stronger fit.
3. These are the people FIGSY *can* contact — you decide who actually gets enrolled
   in the next step.

---

## Step 5 — Launch your first campaign

**Why:** This is the moment FIGSY goes to work — personalised emails in your voice,
sent and followed up automatically, with replies routed back to you.

1. Go to **FIGSY**
2. Create a campaign (it pre-fills from your ICP)
3. **Review the email sequence FIGSY drafts** — edit the voice until it sounds like you
4. Choose **Co-pilot mode** if you want to approve each send before it goes out
   (recommended for your first campaign), or let it run automatically
5. Enrol your leads and launch

> Anyone who replies "not interested" or opts out is **automatically removed** and
> never contacted again. That's handled for you.

---

## Step 6 — Invite your team (optional)

**Why:** If colleagues need to see the pipeline or manage outreach, add them.

1. Go to **Team**
2. Click **Invite teammate**, enter their email
3. They get an invite link and land in the same workspace

---

## What happens next

- **Replies come back to you** — interested ones are flagged.
- **A meeting books** → Denise can confirm it, handle objections, and draft a proposal.
- **Milla** watches everything and gives you a short "what's working" read.

You stay human where it counts. The team handles the repetitive 90%.
