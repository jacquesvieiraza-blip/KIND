# ░ 📮 THE SENDING SPINE — the reason we cannot onboard a paying client (26 Jul · #547–#560) ░
*Minted 26 Jul after the founder pushed back on "nothing is stopping you". **#211 is the parent** and has been 🔴 since 30 Jun; these are its parts, each small enough to be built and verified on its own. Every claim below was read in the code this session, not remembered.*

**The finding, in one paragraph.** `apps/api/src/lib/figsy.ts:26` is `const FROM = COLD_FROM` — a **module-level constant**, used by both `resend.emails.send` calls (≈707 and ≈1096), so every client on the platform emails from the same address. `client_inboxes` exists (`apps/api/src/migrations/20260725_client_inboxes.sql`) and Vida's assign route does **one insert** into it, but the table is **read nowhere on any send path** — only by `demo-mbf.ts`, `pending-migrations.ts` and `routes/operator.ts`. Instantly is not integrated. Replies all arrive through **one** Resend inbound webhook. So *"sends on your own warmed inbox"* — promised on the website, in `mv-previews/home.html`, in `flow-vida.html` and in the pitch — **does not exist**, and a client who pays cannot be delivered.
