The Opportunity: Service Pros + Bundled Lead Discovery

The Core Problem

Service professionals are busy working — on client sites, on shoots, in the field. They can't hunt for leads, answer the phone every time it rings, or follow up via email. Yet they NEED a constant pipeline to stay booked and profitable.

The K.I.N.D Solution: FIGSY (All-In-One) + Agents (MRR)

🔍1. FIGSY Finds + Sends (All One Price)

One bundled cost covers lead discovery, scoring, delivery, AND email sending. Buy 20 FIGSY tokens @ $60 = 20 leads found + 20 personalized emails sent. No double-charging for leads separately.

💬2. Vida Captures Inbound

Website chatbot qualifies hot leads 24/7. Merges inbound + outbound into one unified, scored pipeline.

✉️3. Milla Drafts Outreach

Personalizes the first email per lead (read by FIGSY, written by Milla). Goal: get a reply, not a booking.

📧4. Denise Follows Up (Warm)

Hot reply detected → Denise drafts warm follow-up. Suggests a call. Auto-books a time slot. Includes photo/quote preview if applicable.

📱5. You Close the Deal

Milla reminds you who called. You jump on the call, confirm the job, set the date. Denise has already done the hard work of proving you're responsive.
