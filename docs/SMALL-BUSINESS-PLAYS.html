The Opportunity: Service Pros on a Treadmill

The Core Problem

Service professionals (electricians, plumbers, photographers, etc.) are busy working — on client sites, on shoots, in the field. They can't hunt for leads, answer the phone every time it rings, or follow up via email. Yet they NEED a constant pipeline to stay booked.

The K.I.N.D Solution: 5-Step Automation

🔍1. FIGSY Finds Business

Automated lead discovery using Apollo. Query: "Commercial property managers in [city]" or "Wedding couples in [area]" or "Business owners with old electrical systems." Get 500+ qualified prospects, scored 0–100, delivered at $1/lead.

💬2. Vida Captures Inbound

Website chatbot asks qualifying questions: budget, timeline, scope, pain points. Hot inbound leads are scored and routed. Outbound leads (FIGSY finds them) are merged with inbound into one unified pipeline.

✉️3. Milla Drafts Outreach

Personalized first email per lead (not a template). Milla reads the lead's business and writes something personal: "[Company]'s [Address] looks like it could use [service]…" Goal: get a reply, not a booking.

📧4. FIGSY Sends + Denise Follows Up

FIGSY sends the sequence (Day 0, 4, 9). Hot reply detected → Denise drafts warm follow-up. Suggests a call. Auto-books a time slot. Includes photo/quote preview if applicable.

📱5. You Close the Deal

Milla reminds you who called. You jump on the call, confirm the job, set the date. Denise has already done the hard work of proving you're responsive and worth their time.
