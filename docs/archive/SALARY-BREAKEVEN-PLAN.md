## 🎯 The target
**£75,000/year gross ≈ £4,500/month net ≈ ~£8,000/month in sales.** *(You keep ~91–92% margin, so revenue ≈ what you need plus a small buffer for costs + FX.)*

## 2️⃣ 🩸 The churn truth — why retention IS the salary
Reaching the number is a one-time climb. **Holding it means replacing every client who leaves — forever.**

*Per-lead framing: "churn" = a client who **stops buying leads**; retention = clients who **keep buying leads** month after month (repeat purchase, not a subscription renewal).*

| Book (repeat buyers at ~£155/mo lead spend) | **5%/mo stop buying** | **3%/mo stop buying** |
|---|---|---|
| **YOU** — 52 clients | ~**3/mo** go quiet → must sign ~3/mo to stand still | ~**1.6/mo** |
| **PARTNER** — 202 clients | ~**10/mo** go quiet → a full-time sales machine *forever* | ~**6/mo** |

- **Lower churn is the cheat code:** 5% → 3% nearly **halves** the treadmill.
- **The value of repeat purchase, concretely:** a 52-client book buying ~£155/mo of leads = ~£8,060/mo. **Every 1% who stop buying = ~£80/mo of repeat revenue gone (and compounding).** Cutting the stop-buying rate from 5%→3% protects ~£150/mo of repeat spend and saves re-acquiring **~12 clients/year**.
- **Sign 0 new clients and let repeat purchases lapse, and you fall *below* salary every month.** The book is never "done."

## 3️⃣ 🛠️ The plan — concrete
**For YOU (fastest path to £75k):**
1. **Target ~50 clients spending ~£155/mo on leads** — push FIGSY work + the **+$1 Milla / +$1 Denise layers** on every reveal-only client so revenue-per-client (leads × layers) stays high (fewer logos to chase).
2. **Sign ~5–6/month** for ~10–12 months → you're at salary.
3. **Then defend it:** keep churn **under 3%**, keep signing **~2–3/month** to stay ahead of the bleed.

**For a PARTNER:**
1. **Not a day-one full income** — it's a *stacking* layer (every 10 clients who keep buying leads ≈ +£300/month, for as long as they buy).
2. **Sell teams, not seats** — company clients at heavy lead volume (~$600/mo spend) → **~67 clients, not ~500**.
3. Full-salary partner = **~70–200 steadily-buying clients (by lead volume + layers) + signing ~6–10/month forever** = a full-time agency. Realistic play: KIND commission **on top of** their existing business.
