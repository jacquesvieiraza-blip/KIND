What stays the same, and what happens behind the screens

Stays the same

  • Every safety step (R208 ③): the client approves the direction and the wording in Milla, you approve the wording in Vida, the sender is checked, and the programme is frozen before anything sends.
  • House keeps its own path and its own words.
  • A paying client sees exactly what they see today.

Behind the screens

  • Turning Boutique on is saved with who and when, and is refused if the client has any payment on record (R208 ②).
  • For a Boutique client, P1 and P2 can be authorised internally, with no Stripe payment, no invoice and no revenue (R208 ①).
  • Every payment page is refused for a Boutique client, so they can’t pay a programme price by mistake.
  • The $700 comes from the one price in the shared code that the website already uses (working rule 7), never typed on a screen.

How it ships

This is bigger than I first said. The price shows on five Milla screens, not two. So block 1 goes as three PRs, in this order. Each is tested on its own, and you merge each one.

  1. Turn Boutique on: the Vida box, saved with who and when, refused if the client has paid, with a test that proves the refusal works. Nothing else changes yet.
  2. The free start: P1 and P2 without payment for Boutique clients, payment pages refused for them, and Vida’s lifecycle line. House’s three lock tests change with it, plus a new test that proves a client who isn’t Boutique still can’t start free.
  3. Milla shows your terms: the five Milla screens above.

Please don’t turn Boutique on for a real client until all three are merged and we’ve walked it together.

Two things for you to decide

1. Turning Boutique off again

My recommendation: you can turn it off until P1 is authorised. After that it locks, because the free start has been used, and the box says “Locked: the free start has been used.”

2. “Qualified meetings” next to “per held meeting”

Today Milla counts booked meetings against the target. The Boutique price is per held meeting. My recommendation: keep “qualified meetings” for the target now, and switch Boutique clients to “held meetings” in block 3, when Vida gets “Mark held” and there is a held count to show.
