Rebuilt around the new commercial truth: targeted qualified meetings → recommended people to find → automatic volume price → 50% before sourcing → 50% before Make Live. No $4-per-lead engine and no 7:1 sourcing fiction.
Hard planning rules: $450 per qualified meeting on the R81 curve, paid 50/50 (R141) · 250 people per meeting is the PLAN, 400 is the LIMIT (R136) · a qualified meeting we do not deliver is credited (R136) · Apollo is the only data provider (R146 — PDL and Hunter retired) · partners FROZEN (R139) — partner maths kept for later · target ~70% contribution margin · client actuals replace the 250 plan over time.
v2 — an IDEA being explored, not the live rules. Client pays in full before we run (live rule R141: 50/50) · we stop at a cap of people per meeting (live rule R136: 400) · if the cap is hit the client widens their target or we stop · missed qualified meetings become wallet credit, never a refund · credit is given once only and expires after 90 days · a meeting counts under the 7-point Qualified Meeting rules (attendance not guaranteed). The 250 benchmark is for one qualified meeting, not one paying client. This tab derives prospects per paying client from an explicit meeting → client close rate. It is standalone and does not alter programme pricing, partner economics, company profit or cash.
Canonical rule: Prospects per paying client = leads per booked meeting ÷ meeting-to-client close rate. Example: 250 ÷ 20% = 1,250 prospects → 5 booked meetings → 1 paying client.
