Two prices, one product: Founders vs Enterprise

A new founder will never pay $450 a meeting. An enterprise will doubt anything at $99. So each audience gets its own price. Enterprise gets more, not just a higher price, and the whole programme is protected so it can never lose money.
💡 IDEA — nothing here is decided or live. Today's live rules: $450 on the site (R141), paid 50/50 (R141), limit 400 people per meeting (R136).

In their ICP · right role · agreed to meet · date and time set · genuinely relevant · not an existing customer · acceptance evidenced. Attendance is not guaranteed.
