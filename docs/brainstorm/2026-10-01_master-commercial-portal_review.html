Your decisions: 6 questions

Answer these and the build is defined. Reply e.g. "Q1 yes", or "all yes" to take every recommendation.

  1. Q1
    Can Coaching follow a meeting past "booked"?
    Deal Coach, Review Room, Win/Loss and Conversion Intelligence all need to know what happens after the meeting. Today "meeting booked" is your hard line.
    My recommendation: Yes, for Coaching only. The programme still promises booked meetings.
  2. Q2
    How is the +$100 Coaching uplift paid?
    The programme is one payment up front. Coaching from 50% onwards needs a second charge.
    My recommendation: At activation, one payment of $100 × the meetings still to come. If a meeting isn't delivered, the $100 is credited back with it.
  3. Q3
    Call recordings: now or later?
    5 of the 15 Coaching features need the client's own call recordings: call transcription, Scorecards, Rep Coaching, Team Coaching, Winning Moments. That is a new vendor plus legal consent work.
    My recommendation: Later. Build everything that works from Milla's "How did it go?" debrief first. Recordings are Phase 3.
  4. Q4
    Does every plan get "How did it go?" after a meeting?
    Growth includes follow-up drafts and learning, which are impossible without knowing what happened. Your table puts Debrief in Full Coaching only.
    My recommendation: Yes. A one-tap outcome for every plan; the deep debrief stays in Full Coaching.
  5. Q5
    Call the 25/50/75 engine "Product 4"?
    R39 numbers products by what clients buy (1 Pipeline, 2 Coaching, 3 SaaS). Nobody buys the milestones; they are how we sell.
    My recommendation: Call them "Expansion moments", a feature of Milla, not a product.
  6. Q6
    Build inside today's look?
    The concept's menu and layout differ from live Milla, and you locked the look (R167).
    My recommendation: Yes. Coaching's tabs go inside the existing Coaching page, and the offers use the existing right-hand panel.

1

Verdict

The model is strong and coherent. Same meetings for everyone. Coaching grows with the plan. A simple ladder ($99 → $199, $199 → $299, $299 all-in). Milestones that fire on delivery, once, and remember the answer. It never repeats a "declined" offer and never sells Enterprise what it already owns. The tone is honest throughout ("not enough evidence yet", no invented ROI). That is exactly what your rules ask for.

The big thing it hides: about a third of the Coaching features need recordings of the client's own sales calls, and some need several reps per client. We have neither today. Those are the biggest builds and carry legal work. The good news: everything you need to sell Coaching at 50% and deliver real value works without recordings (Phase 1 below).

Three rule clashes to settle (Q1–Q2, Q6): Coaching going past "meeting booked", how the +$100 is paid when the programme is paid up front, and building inside today's locked look.

The 7 foundations underneath

Most sections are small once these exist. They are the real build.

  • F1
    Meeting outcome capture M

    After each meeting Milla asks "How did it go?": held / no-show / next step agreed / not now / lost, plus one line of what happened. Held and no-show confirmation already exists (meeting truth); the rest is new.

    Unlocks: Every plan

  • F2
    Milestone engine (25 / 50 / 75 / 100%) M

    Delivered qualified meetings ÷ purchased target. Fires once per programme, stores the client's answer (viewed / engaged / accepted / not now / declined / already included). Re-uses today's meetings-against-target count.

    Unlocks: 25/50/75 rooms

  • F3
    Coaching activation + billing M

    Turn Full Coaching on, stamp the date, charge +$100 for each remaining meeting. Today the programme is ONE payment up front (R166), so this needs a second payment for the remaining meetings. Prices from the shared price settings.

    Unlocks: Founders + Growth upgrade

  • F6
    The client's own sales context M

    One conversation with Milla: what they sell, their pitch, proof/case studies, usual objections, what a won deal looks like. Stored once, used by every Coaching feature.

    Unlocks: Pitch-aware prep, objections, roleplay

  • F7
    Outcomes past "booked" M

    Opportunity → proposal → won / lost, supplied by the client in Milla (later: from their CRM). Goes past today's hard line of "meeting booked" (rule 21), so it needs your word.

    Unlocks: Deal Coach, Review Room, Win/Loss, Conversion Intelligence

  • F5
    Teams: several people per client L

    Reps and a manager under one client, each with their own meetings. An old seat model exists; organisation buyers are parked (#2164).

    Unlocks: Team Coaching, Manager View, Rep Coaching

  • F4
    Call recordings / transcripts XL

    Recording or transcribing the client's own sales calls (Zoom / Teams / Meet, or upload). New vendor, storage, and legal work: consent to record, POPIA/GDPR, some US states need everyone on the call to agree. We capture none of this today.

    Unlocks: Debrief transcription, Scorecards, Rep/Team Coaching, Winning Moments

Layout review

These are about the concept file itself. The real build uses the live Milla components, so they're notes for the build, not faults in your thinking.

  • Text is very small. Most labels are 6–9px (about 130 pieces of text). Readable on your big screen, not on a laptop or phone. The live product uses larger text, so build from the real components, not this file's sizes.
  • Hidden tabs. Enterprise has 16 tabs in a strip with the scrollbar hidden. Only about 8 show, and nothing tells you the rest exist.
  • Wrong highlight. The left menu always highlights "Home", and the stage bar always sits on "Results". That stays the same even on the Brief, Proof, Approval and Deal Coach screens. In the real build the highlight must follow the page.
  • "Included" everywhere. Every list row ends in an "Included" badge. That includes offer screens listing what is NOT included yet, and trigger screens where it means nothing.
  • Same 3 buttons. "Show me more · What happens next? · Explain this" are identical on all 56 screens. Real quick replies should fit each screen (e.g. "Draft the follow-up", "Show the evidence").
  • Phone. The portal is three fixed columns, so on a phone it is cut off. Live Milla already switches on a phone (Milla is the screen; sections open over her). Coaching must use that.
  • Menu names differ from live. Concept: Home · Pipeline · Meetings · Coaching · Replies / Profile · Results · Account. Live: Pipeline · Meetings · Programme · Inbox / My ICP · Documents · Reports · Coaching. You locked the current look (R167), so Coaching's new pages go inside the existing Coaching page as tabs.

Wording review

What's right and should be kept

  • "Unknown remains unknown" and "not enough evidence yet": Milla never invents a conclusion.
  • "No invented ROI, outcomes or unsupported claims" is written into Follow-Up and Win/Loss.
  • All prices in $, per qualified meeting, and no prospect counts or sourcing rates shown (R136).
  • "I'm not going to sell it to you again" (Enterprise 50%): the tone you want.
  • "Never rank reps by a vanity score": strong principle for Team Coaching.

What to change

  • Free Proof: "12 examples" → 20 (what live Proof shows).
  • Founders Reporting at 40%: remove "you need Full Coaching for the deeper conversion analysis". It's a sales line before your 50% moment.
  • Upgrade screens: the "Included" badge on what-unlocks rows → "Unlocks".
  • Enterprise overview: drop "Expansion Engine · Active 25/50/75". It's our machinery, not the client's.
  • "Draft pricing / Draft commercial model": our note, never shown to a client.
  • Meeting prep "Attendee context": we only know the person who booked. Add attendees or drop the line.
  • Trigger Event + Response State screens (25/50/75): move to Vida. A client never sees the trigger.
  • Conversion Intelligence "client / CRM supplied": say "you tell Milla" until a CRM connection exists.
  • Win/Loss "enough closed outcomes": set the number (e.g. 10) so Milla knows when to speak.
  • Quick-reply buttons: write them per screen instead of the same three everywhere.
