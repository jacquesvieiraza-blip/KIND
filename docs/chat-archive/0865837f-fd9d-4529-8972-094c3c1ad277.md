The Speed Pipeline for Lead Gen works like this — end to end, no manual steps:

**Hour 0 — Signup & onboard**
- Client signs up, confirms email, lands on onboarding
- They enter their website URL → "Pre-fill ICP" button calls Claude, which scrapes the site and pre-populates all ICP fields in under 10 seconds
- Client reviews/tweaks and saves

**Hour 0–1 — Automatic search**
- The moment they hit "Save & Find Leads", `runIcpJob` fires in the background immediately (no manual Run button)
- It calls Apollo with `apollo_only_consented: true` — this filters for contacts where email status is `verified` or `likely_to_engage`, meaning they're pre-consented and contactable right now, no waiting for consent emails
- Apollo returns up to 50 matched contacts per search

**Hour 0–1 — Scoring & notification**
- Leads are inserted into the DB, then Claude haiku scores all of them in batches of 10 — one AI call per 10 leads
- The moment the first batch is inserted, a "Your leads are ready" email fires to the client automatically
- Client clicks through to see ranked, scored leads with reasoning

**The result**: Client goes from "just signed up" to "scored leads in their dashboard with an email notification" in under 2 hours, sometimes under 30 minutes if Apollo responds fast.

The bottleneck is really Apollo's API response time and whether the client's ICP is specific enough. The `apollo_only_consented` flag is the key unlock — without it you'd need to wait days for consent emails to be replied to before you can do anything with a lead.
