## NEW SECTION — PRODUCT ROADMAP: FULL AGENT AUTONOMY

---

### FIGSY — What "Fully Autonomous" Means

**Where FIGSY is today:**
- Executes autonomously once a campaign is set up (sends every 2 hours, self-pauses, weekly digest, at-risk alerts)
- Campaign creation, ICP definition, and lead search still require a human

**What full autonomy looks like:**

**1. Self-Starting Campaigns**
FIGSY detects when a client's pipeline is running low — e.g., fewer than X active leads in sequence — and automatically suggests or starts a new campaign using the client's existing ICP profile. No human needs to notice the pipeline is thinning.

**2. Self-Improving Messaging**
FIGSY analyses which email subjects, openers, and follow-up patterns are generating the highest reply rates across campaigns. It automatically updates its own templates based on what's working — without a human reviewing the data.

**3. Self-Scheduling Lead Searches**
Currently a user triggers a lead search. Fully autonomous FIGSY would run its own lead search on a schedule — weekly or bi-weekly — replenishing the lead pool without being asked.

**4. Autonomous Objection Routing**
When FIGSY classifies a reply as an objection, it already sends a differentiation response. Full autonomy extends this: FIGSY decides whether to continue the sequence, escalate to the human, or close the thread — based on reply tone and content — without waiting for instruction.

**5. Full Campaign Lifecycle Without Human Input**
The goal state: a client sets their ICP once, approves a tone/style guide once, and FIGSY runs indefinitely — sourcing leads, sequencing them, adjusting messaging, pausing underperformers, and restarting with refreshed leads — end to end.

**What still needs a human (even fully autonomous):**
- ICP definition at onboarding (once)
- Tone/brand voice approval (once)
- Compliance sign-off (POPIA consent mechanisms remain human-approved)

---

### Milla — What "Fully Autonomous" Means

**Where Milla is today:**
- Reactive RAG-based Q&A — you ask, she answers using your live pipeline data
- No proactive output whatsoever

**What full autonomy looks like:**

**1. Daily Morning Intelligence Brief**
Every morning at a set time, Milla sends the client a short email or in-app notification: "Here's what changed in your pipeline overnight. 3 new interested leads. Reply rate this week: 5.4% vs. 3% industry avg. 1 campaign needs attention." No prompting required.

**2. Proactive Anomaly Alerts**
Milla monitors key metrics continuously and fires an alert when something changes significantly — reply rate drops below threshold, a campaign goes quiet, a hot lead hasn't been followed up within 48 hours, credit balance is running low. Sent via email or in-app notification, not waiting for the client to open a dashboard.

**3. Weekly Pipeline Summary (Client-Facing)**
Beyond the founder digest that already runs on Mondays, Milla generates a personalised client-facing summary: what happened this week, what's working, what to focus on next. Pushed to the client without them asking.

**4. Proactive Recommendations**
Milla identifies patterns — e.g., "Your Tuesday sends have a 40% higher reply rate than Friday sends" or "Leads from fintech have 2× your average score" — and surfaces these as actionable insights unprompted. Not buried in a dashboard. Pushed to the client.

**5. "Today's Intelligence" Dashboard Feed**
When a client opens their dashboard, Milla has already populated a feed of the 3–5 most important things happening in their pipeline right now — without the client asking a single question. The dashboard opens with intelligence, not empty charts.

**The distinction between Milla today and Milla autonomous:**
- Today: Milla is a very smart search engine. You query it.
- Autonomous: Milla is a business analyst who sends you a brief every morning and taps you on the shoulder when something matters.
