2. K.I.N.D receives the reply centrally
3. Claude reads it and classifies it — positive, negative, or ambiguous
4. **Negative** → opt-out recorded, blocklist updated, no credit used, no action
5. **Ambiguous** → flagged for review (either by admin or a second AI pass)
6. **Positive** → 1 credit consumed, lead status flips to "qualified", client is notified instantly (email + in-app), the full email thread appears in the client's dashboard

The client never misses a positive reply because the system catches it and pushes it to them. They don't need to monitor an inbox — they just get a notification that says "New qualified lead — [Name] at [Company] replied positively."

In the admin portal you see all of this across every client — total emails sent, reply classifications, qualified leads, credits consumed. Full visibility.
