**Now — what "polish" actually means:**

Polish is the gap between a site that *works* and a site that *converts*. It's not decoration. It's removing every moment of friction or doubt a buyer feels as they scroll. Here's what it means specifically for KIND:

---

**1. Visual consistency**
Every page should feel like it was built by one hand. Right now the nav has a blue logo icon (`--blue: #0066FF`) but the rest of the site is purple. That one inconsistency signals "unfinished" to a buyer's brain. Fix: swap the logo icon to purple.

**2. Copy tightness**
Every sentence that could be two sentences should be one. Every paragraph that could be a line should be a line. Buyers scan — if the first 5 words of a sentence don't grab them, they skip it. The agent benefit copy on the cards is already good. The "Why KIND Pillars" section is slightly wordy.

**3. Trust signals in the right places**
Right now POPIA is only in the trust bar (which hides on mobile). It should appear again near the pricing CTA — that's the moment of highest anxiety. A one-liner "POPIA compliant · No contracts · Cancel anytime" directly under the buy button removes the last blocker.

**4. Mobile experience**
The site looks good on desktop. On mobile the hero agent cards stack, the trust bar disappears, and the promise strip collapses. Those are fine — but the CTA buttons need to be full-width on mobile, and the nav dropdown should be a hamburger. Worth a pass.

**5. Micro-interactions**
Hover states on every clickable element. The agent cards already have shadow + pause-on-hover. The pricing cards should subtly lift on hover. Buttons should have a 150ms ease transition on background. These cost nothing to build and make the site feel alive.

**6. Proof as it arrives**
The logo strip is empty now. The moment you have one client, even a friend-of-a-founder, add their logo. One real logo beats ten empty slots. Same with testimonials — one real quote, however short, outperforms any copy you write about yourself.

**7. Page speed**
The agent PNGs (1024×1536) are being served full-size. Compress them to WebP with a 600px max-width version for mobile. Faster load = lower bounce = more conversions.

**Bottom line:** Polish means every element earns its place, every word earns its weight, and no buyer leaves with an unanswered question. It's an ongoing practice, not a one-time task.
