# POPIA Information Officer — registration checklist and duties

## 1. Who the Information Officer is

**By default, the head of the private body — i.e. the founder.** POPIA does not require an
appointment for the default position to hold: the head of a private body **is** the Information
Officer automatically. An appointment is only needed to designate someone else, and deputies are
designated in addition, never instead.
