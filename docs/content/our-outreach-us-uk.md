## 1. WHO — the ICP (start narrow, expand on data)
**Primary:** B2B companies that **already do (or want) outbound but have no SDR capacity.**
- **Size:** ~5–50 employees (growth-stage; big enough to have a sales motion, small enough to feel the SDR-hire cost).
- **Type:** B2B SaaS · agencies / consultancies · professional & B2B services · recruiters.
- **Geo (legal-first — see compliance §5):** **US · UK · Ireland · France · Netherlands.** *(Avoid Germany/Poland for cold until consent-grade.)*
- **Titles:** Founder / CEO (sub-20) · Head of Sales / Sales Director · Head of Growth / Revenue · Commercial Director.
- **Trigger signals (sharpen with 139/Apollo playbook):** hiring an SDR/BDR (job post) · recently raised · "we do outbound" in content · agency listing clients.

**⭐ Dual-purpose angle:** agencies fit BOTH our client ICP *and* the **partner** ICP (🅲 track) — if they'd rather resell than buy, route them to PARTNER-BRIEF. One list, two outcomes.

**Anti-ICP (skip):** B2C-only · <5 people with no sales function · enterprise (long cycles — not the fast-cash motion) · German/Polish cold (compliance).

## 3. THE OFFER / ANGLE
**The hook = the dogfood proof.** We're not claiming AI SDRs are magic (the market's sick of that) — we're *showing* it: this email was sent by our own AI SDR, personalized, and it found you. The offer:
> *"An AI sales rep that books meetings for B2B teams without an SDR hire — and you're reading proof it works."*
- **CTA (low-friction):** a 15-min demo, OR a free sample — "want me to build your ICP + draft your first 10 outbound emails free, so you can see the quality?" *(the free sample = the strongest CTA; it IS the product.)*
- **Brand rule (the backlash antidote):** honest, quality-not-volume, human-in-the-loop. Never "fully autonomous, replace your team." (Matches the blog + the AI-SDR-backlash finding.)
