Demo Walkthrough & Talk Track

The full client-facing demo, broken into elements — what's on screen, exactly what to say, and why it lands. Use it to learn the talk track and as the script for your onboarding videos.

🎬 12 scenes · ~8 min 🎙 Verbatim talk track 📹 Onboarding-video ready

How to use this

  • Each scene = one screen / one idea. Learn them one at a time.
  • Say this is a starting script — make it yours, don't read it robotically.
  • For onboarding videos, record one scene per clip — short, focused, re-shoot easily.

Part 1 · The Hook
1

Open with the problem

0:00 – 0:30 · Black screen or your face
📺 On screen
You, talking to camera, or a clean title card. No product yet.
🎙 Say this
"Every business needs more meetings in the calendar. But finding the right people, writing the emails, following up, spotting who's actually interested — it's a full-time job most small teams don't have time for. So what if you had a team that did it for you — for the price of a couple of coffees a month? That's K.I.N.D. Let me show you."
✅ Why it lands
Name the pain first. Don't open with features — open with the thing keeping them up at night: an empty pipeline.
💡 Tip
Keep it under 30 seconds. Energy high. Don't say "AI" yet — say "a team."
2

Meet your AI revenue team

0:30 – 1:00 · Dashboard / agents
📺 On screen
The dashboard with the agent cards — FIGSY, Milla, Vida, Denise.
🎙 Say this
"This is your team. FIGSY is your opener — she finds leads and reaches out. Milla is your brain — she knows your business and answers anything. Vida is your connector — she handles your website chat. And Denise is your closer. They each have a name and a job, just like real teammates — because that's how they work for you."
✅ Why it lands
The named, human team is the differentiator. It's warm where competitors are cold "agent platforms." Lead with that.
💡 Tip
Point at each card as you name them. Make them feel like people.
Part 2 · Getting started
3

Sign up in seconds

1:00 – 1:30 · Onboarding chat
📺 On screen
The signup → FIGSY onboarding chat. A few quick questions: company, what you do, country, website.
🎙 Say this
"Signing up takes seconds. No long forms — FIGSY just asks a few questions like a real conversation: who you are, what you do, where your customers are. And you don't need anyone's approval to start. Drop in your website and she'll even read it to learn your business."
✅ Why it lands
"Seconds to start, no approval needed" is the anti-enterprise message — your $20 self-serve edge. Say it out loud.
4

Build your ICP — just talk to FIGSY

1:30 – 2:30 · ICP Builder
📺 On screen
ICP Builder. Type a plain-English target into FIGSY: e.g. "SaaS revenue leaders in the US and UK, 50–500 staff." Watch the form fill in.
🎙 Say this
"Now we tell FIGSY who your ideal customer is — and you can just say it. Watch: 'VPs of Sales and Revenue at SaaS companies, 50 to 500 people, US and UK.' She turns that into a real targeting profile automatically. No spreadsheets, no Boolean filters."
✅ Why it lands
The "just talk to it" moment removes the scariest part of prospecting tools. Show the form auto-filling — that's the wow.
💡 Tip
Have your example phrase ready. Let the live preview count appear ("X matching leads") — it's real proof.
Part 3 · The product working
5

Find leads in one click

2:30 – 3:30 · Run ICP → People
📺 On screen
Click Run ICP → "FIGSY is sourcing your leads…" → People page fills with real, named leads, each with a score and a verified email.
🎙 Say this
"One click. FIGSY goes out, finds real people who match, and brings them back — scored, so you start with the warmest, and with a verified email so you can actually reach them. This is the part that normally takes a junior rep a whole afternoon."
✅ Why it lands
Real names + real emails + a score = "this is a working product, not a demo." Click into one lead to show the email is real.
💡 Tip
Pre-run it just before recording so leads appear fast — sourcing takes a minute in the background.
6

Your scored pipeline

3:30 – 4:00 · People page
📺 On screen
Hover the scores and signals — "High fit", "Decision maker", "Hiring". Show the score column 0–100.
🎙 Say this
"Every lead is graded out of 100 and tagged with why they're a good fit — decision-maker, growing team, right industry. So you're never guessing who to talk to first. FIGSY does the qualifying; you get a ranked list."
✅ Why it lands
Scoring = "we do the thinking." It reframes a list of names into a prioritised plan of action.
7

FIGSY writes & sends

4:00 – 5:00 · Campaign
📺 On screen
A campaign with a 3-step sequence (Day 0, Day 4, Day 9). Open a generated email — short, personalised, human.
🎙 Say this
"Now FIGSY writes the outreach — in your voice, personalised to each person, not a template. A first email, then a couple of follow-ups if they don't reply. Here's a real one she wrote. Short. Human. No 'hope this finds you well.' She sends it for you and follows up automatically."
✅ Why it lands
Show a real generated email. The quality of the copy sells itself — let them read it for a beat.
💡 Tip
Pick a clean, well-written sample ahead of time. Mention follow-ups stop the moment someone replies.
8

The magic moment — a reply becomes a hot lead

5:00 – 6:00 · Inbox
📺 On screen
The Inbox. A reply appears, auto-classified 🔥 Hot, with an AI draft reply ready and a Mark meeting booked button.
🎙 Say this
"And this is the part that matters. When someone replies, FIGSY reads it, decides if they're interested, and flags the hot ones for you — right here. You don't dig through an inbox. The interested people come to you, marked hot, with a reply already drafted. All you do is hit send and book the call."
✅ Why it lands
This is the emotional peak of the demo — "the interested people come to you." Slow down. Let it breathe.
💡 Tip
If possible, show a real hot reply landing live. It's the single most convincing moment in the whole demo.
9

Book the meeting

6:00 – 6:30 · Booking
📺 On screen
Connect a calendar / the booking link in the email; "Mark meeting booked" updating the KPI.
🎙 Say this
"Connect your calendar and your booking link goes straight into the emails, so a hot lead can book a time in one click. When a meeting's booked, it counts it for you — so you can see your pipeline growing in real numbers."
✅ Why it lands
Close the loop: leads → reply → meeting booked. That's the outcome they're buying. Tie it back to scene 1's promise.
Part 4 · The bigger picture
10

The rest of the team

6:30 – 7:30 · Milla · Vida · Denise
📺 On screen
Quick flashes: Milla answering a question from uploaded docs; Vida's chat bubble on a website; Denise's closer panel.
🎙 Say this
"FIGSY opens the door — but she's not alone. Milla knows your business inside out: ask her anything, she drafts documents and answers from your own knowledge. Vida sits on your website catching visitors and turning them into leads. And Denise, your closer, takes a warm lead and helps carry it over the line. One subscription, a whole revenue team."
✅ Why it lands
Show breadth without losing focus — FIGSY is the hero, the others are "and there's more." Keep each to ~15 seconds.
💡 Tip
Don't deep-dive here. A taste each. The full demo of Milla/Vida/Denise can be its own onboarding clip.
11

Start for $20

7:30 – 8:00 · Pricing
📺 On screen
Pricing / billing — the $20 entry, pay-as-you-go credits, clear and simple.
🎙 Say this
"Here's the part no one else can say: you can start for $20. No big contract, no procurement, no sales call to get in. You pay for results as you grow. Try it on a small list this week and see real leads come back."
✅ Why it lands
"$20, no approval" is your one ownable number. End the value story on it — it removes the last objection.
12

Close & call to action

8:00 – 8:30 · Your face / CTA card
📺 On screen
Back to you, or a clean CTA card with the URL: app.get-kind.com.
🎙 Say this
"So that's K.I.N.D — a revenue team that finds your leads, reaches out, spots the interested ones, and books you the meetings. You close the deals. We'll bring you the meetings. Start today at app.get-kind.com — it takes two minutes."
✅ Why it lands
End on the brand line and one clear action. One CTA only. Don't trail off — finish strong.
💡 Tip
Smile on the last line. The last 5 seconds are what they remember.
📝 Founder polish pass: tighten any line that feels scripted, swap [brackets] for real values, and cut anything that doesn't earn its place. Aim for energy + clarity over completeness. Re-record scene-by-scene until each clip feels natural.
