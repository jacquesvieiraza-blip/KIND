## The core shift

**From:** "AI-powered lead generation" (a tool — competes on features/price)
**To:** "Your AI revenue team" (a team — competes on outcome and relationship)

Tools get compared on a spec sheet. Teams get hired. The whole family story
(FIGSY opens, Milla thinks, Vida connects, Denise closes) only makes sense
under the "team" frame — so the positioning and the product finally agree.

We are deliberately NOT borrowing the literal phrases competitors own:
- ❌ "Clone your best humans" (Atlas) · ❌ "60 seconds" (Atlas) ·
  ❌ "Turn followers into revenue" (Revio) · ❌ Glean's "platform/layer" narrative.
- ✅ Our frame: a *named team that learns your business and shares one memory.*
