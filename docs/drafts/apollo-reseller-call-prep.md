## 0. The 10-second frame (memorise this — say it early, repeat it)
> *"Apollo is the data-and-tooling platform that sales teams operate themselves. K.I.N.D is an autonomous AI sales team for African SMBs that have no sales team. We don't sell data — we **consume** it to deliver outcomes, for customers who would never buy or operate Apollo directly. So we expand your consumption, we don't compete for your buyers."*

Everything below is just evidence for that sentence.

## 1. What K.I.N.D is (30-second version)
- An **AI sales platform for African B2B SMBs** (~5–50 people) who can't afford a real sales team.
- A family of AI agents that run the whole revenue loop **done-for-you**:
  - **FIGSY** — AI SDR: finds leads, writes & sends 3-step cold-email sequences, classifies replies, pushes hot ones to CRM.
  - **Vida** — website/WhatsApp chatbot that qualifies inbound visitors.
  - **Denise** — AI closer: warm follow-ups & proposals.
  - **Milla** — business-intelligence assistant. **Casey** — onboarding. **Lena** — customer success.
- **Outcome pricing:** customers pay per *result*, not per record or seat. **1 credit = 1 positive reply.**
- **Launching Fri 19 Jun**, Africa-first (South Africa → Nigeria → Kenya → Ghana).

## 2. The ask (be explicit)
> *"We'd like a formal data-reseller / wholesale agreement so the way we already consume Apollo to power our service is fully clean and can scale with us."*

## 3. Why there's no competitive overlap (the heart of the call)
Hit these four — each is a different axis of "we're not you":

1. **Different customer / segment.** African SMBs with no sales team. They will **never** sign up for Apollo, build a list, or run a sequence themselves. We bring them into the market Apollo serves → **net-new consumption**, not substitution.
2. **Different layer of the stack.** Apollo = the data + tooling layer the user *operates*. K.I.N.D = the **autonomous execution layer on top** — our agents do the work end-to-end; the customer never touches a data tool.
3. **Different business model.** We charge for **outcomes** (replies/meetings), not for data access or seats. We're not a data marketplace — **data is a small fraction of our cost.**
4. **Multi-source by design.** Apollo is **one premium source** in our stack; we blend it with other providers (PDL, Hunter) to fill African coverage gaps Apollo alone has. It's a wholesale input, never customer-facing.

**The geographic wedge (use it):** Apollo **doesn't take African payments**, and African SMBs won't transact with it directly. K.I.N.D is the local rails that makes Apollo's data reach a continent it currently can't monetise.

## 4. The API-exposure answer (their direct question #2) — say it plainly
> *"The Apollo API is integrated **entirely server-side**. It is **not exposed to customers** — no Apollo workspace, no API access, no list export. Customers only ever receive K.I.N.D's processed output: scored leads and autonomous outreach. We use two endpoints behind the scenes — people search and email enrichment — to power that."*

This is true and it's your strongest position: you're not reselling Apollo *access*, you're consuming Apollo *data* to manufacture a different product.

## 5. Why Apollo should want this (turn it around — give them the upside)
- **New revenue + new TAM:** a whole African SMB segment monetised through you, that Apollo can't reach directly today.
- **Volume grows with us:** outcome model means we pull more data as we grow — a consumption customer, not a one-off.
- **Clean, centralised relationship:** one managed integration vs. chasing individual SMBs.
