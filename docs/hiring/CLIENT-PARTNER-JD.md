# Client Partner — role description

> **Status: R40 (15 Aug 2026).** Commission-only independent contractor. The seat is built and
> turn-key; the founder creates it the day she starts. Nothing about this role appears on the
> public site until the founder says so.

## The role in one line

You bring clients from your own network, and you keep them. You are paid twice for that: once
when a client starts, and every month they stay.

## What the job actually is

**1 · Sell — from your own relationships.**
You work your own network. You do not use our system to find leads, and you are not given a
prospecting list. What you are given is a product that works, a founder who runs the demos,
and a price that is easy to say out loud: **$299 to start, which includes the first 100
approved leads, then $4 per approved lead.**

⚠️ **You do not run demos.** The founder demos, for now. You open the door, qualify the
interest, and bring him in.

**2 · Onboard — the first session decides everything.**
When a client starts, you walk them through their first session personally: their ICP is set,
their first leads are reviewed, their first approvals are made. A client who approves nobody
in week one is a client who leaves in month two. This session is the single highest-value
hour in the whole relationship.

**3 · Retain — be the named human.**
You are the person your clients know. You watch whether approvals are happening, you nudge
the ones who have gone quiet, and you answer the business WhatsApp. When something is wrong
you find out before they tell us. This is where most of your income comes from, which is
deliberate: **keeping clients pays better than churning through new ones.**

**4 · Feed the content bank.**
Every objection, every reason a reply gave, every win — captured verbatim, the same day. Not
a summary written later. This is the raw material the whole business markets from, and you
are the only person hearing it first-hand.

## How you are paid

| | Rate | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Landing a client** | **20%** of the $299 starting pack | Once, when their money is collected |
| **Holding a client** | **8%** of everything that client spends | Every month, for as long as you hold them |

- **Earned when collected** — commission is earned on money that has actually arrived, never
  on a promise or an invoice.
- **No clawback.** Once it is earned it is yours.
- **Paid monthly** via Wise, in rand, against your invoice.
- **No base, no cap, no target pay.** Commission-only means nothing is guaranteed and nothing
  is limited.

Your portal shows your earnings live: what you have earned this month, what is recurring,
which clients you hold, and a forecaster for what a given pace would build to.

## What you can see

Your own earnings, your own clients, and how those clients are doing. **You do not see what a
client spends** — you see your share of it. That is a deliberate boundary, not an oversight.

## What this role is not

- Not employment. You are an independent contractor: your own hours, your own tools, free to
  work with others.
- Not a demo role — the founder demos.
- Not lead-gen for yourself — you bring your own relationships.
- Not authorised to sign, price or promise anything on the company's behalf. Pricing is fixed
  and public; there is no discounting to negotiate.

## Paperwork

A commission agreement and an NDA are signed before the seat goes live. Both live in your
portal under your name, with their status shown, so you always know what is outstanding.
