# Partner conduct policy

**Who this applies to:** every K.I.N.D partner, referrer and commission seller, and anyone acting
on their behalf.

**Why it exists:** partners talk to the market as if they were us. What a partner promises, we
are expected to deliver; what a partner claims, we are judged by. This page is the short list of
things that end the relationship.

## You must not

1. **Pay or offer a kickback or any improper inducement** to win business — cash, gifts,
   hospitality of a kind that would embarrass either of us if it were published, or anything of
   value offered to influence a buying decision improperly. This includes offering to share your
   commission with someone who is deciding whether their employer buys.

2. **Offer anything at all to a public official**, at any level, anywhere, for any reason
   connected to K.I.N.D — money, gifts, hospitality, favours, or the promise of any of them.
   Not small, not customary, not through anyone else. If you think something is a grey area,
   stop and ask before you do it.

3. **Misrepresent K.I.N.D** — what the product does, what it has achieved, who uses it, where
   data is stored, or what we are certified or registered as. If you are not certain a claim is
   true, do not make it. Ask.

4. **Promise terms, prices, discounts or timelines that have not been approved.** Pricing and
   commercial terms come from us. A promise made in a room becomes an expectation we have to
   honour or refuse, and both are damaging.

5. **Use fake testimonials, invented results, or numbers you cannot evidence.** No made-up client
   names, no borrowed case studies, no illustrative figures presented as real outcomes. If a
   number is illustrative, say so in the same breath.

6. **Subcontract selling, or pass a K.I.N.D referral code to anyone else, without our written
   permission.** A code is issued to you personally. Your obligations do not transfer with it.

## You must

- **Keep to the approved offer and the approved words** — the pack you signed, and nothing added.
- **Respect a "no".** If a prospect asks not to be contacted, that is final and immediate, and
  you tell us so it reaches our suppression list.
- **Tell us promptly** if you learn of anything that breaches this policy, including your own
  mistake. Telling us early is treated very differently from us finding out later.
- **Follow the data rules** in the agreement you signed — prospect data you receive from us is
  ours, used only to sell K.I.N.D, and not kept after the relationship ends.

## What happens if you breach it

Depending on what happened: a conversation and a correction; suspension of your referral code;
withholding of commission on affected business; or immediate termination. **Bribery,
impersonation and fabricated results are termination**, and we will report conduct we are
required to report.

⚠️ Note on referral codes: a code stops resolving when a seat is suspended or archived, so
suspension takes effect immediately rather than on paperwork. Seats are archived, never deleted,
so commission already earned survives the relationship ending (R42).
