## SECTION 2 — BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

**What KIND does:**

K.I.N.D Technologies Ltd develops and operates KIND AI, a B2B artificial intelligence sales platform designed specifically for African businesses. The platform delivers three AI-powered sales agents as a service: FIGSY (automated personalised outbound email and LinkedIn prospecting); Milla (strategic lead generation and ICP-matched prospect list building); and Vida (AI-driven inbound web chat agent that qualifies and captures leads from customers' websites).

**Primary market:**

Small and medium-sized enterprises across Sub-Saharan Africa and South Africa engaged in B2B sales, with particular focus on professional services, technology, logistics, financial services, and business process outsourcing. The platform is accessed globally via web application, with primary customer concentration in African markets.

**Revenue model:**

1. Recurring SaaS subscriptions billed via Stripe, providing tiered access to the KIND AI platform on a monthly basis.
2. Credit packs purchased on an as-needed basis via Stripe, allowing customers to purchase additional AI execution credits for high-volume campaigns beyond their subscription tier.

### Important Flags

1. **"Milla" and "Vida" registrability risk** — common given names may face distinctiveness objections. Run a clearance search before filing (~£300–500 for professional search). Consider stylised marks (logo + wordmark) if pure word mark is refused.

2. **File as "KIND" not "K.I.N.D"** — clean wordmark, easier to enforce.

3. **Class 35 specification must be precise** — "lead generation services" alone may be refused as too vague. Work with a trademark attorney on specification language.

4. **African market protection is separate** — UKIPO gives no protection in SA, Nigeria, Kenya, etc. File nationally (CIPC for SA ~ZAR 2,500–3,500/class/mark; KEIPO for Kenya) at Series A or first major African expansion.

5. **Recommended next step:** Commission a clearance search on all four marks in Classes 35, 38, and 42 before filing (~£400–600 total, 5–7 working days). Do not file blind.
