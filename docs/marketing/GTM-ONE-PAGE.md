## 1 · ONE AVATAR

**Founders of agencies and consultancies, ~5–30 staff, referral-dependent. Global — US/UK primary** *(R23, 12 Aug: "Global").*

The one we want is the **systems buyer**: wants a pipeline that behaves predictably. Not the panic buyer chasing this month's gap — that one churns.

**Their real problem:** the people best at winning work are buried in delivery, so business development becomes whoever has a spare hour. Pipeline goes quiet exactly when they get busy.

**Their real fear, and it is not "will it work":** *"will something go out under my name that embarrasses me?"* Most of them have watched a tool or an SDR do precisely that.

**The wedge, one line:**

> ### "You approve every prospect before anyone is contacted."

Competitors sell volume — more sends, faster. **They cannot offer approval without breaking what they sell.** We can, because the client's 👍 is the only thing that spends money.

**The free-10 is yours, not the site's.** Ten leads, run free, **offered by you personally to a named person in a call or a reply** — never on a page, never in a post, never in code *(R1, 6 Aug · R21, 12 Aug: "the free offer is my call when i need it").*

**Our rule of 100 is 100 PEOPLE, not 100 a day.** Five a day, each written by hand, reaches 100 named humans in a month. Hormozi's number assumes templates; ours assumes you actually know them — which is the only reason warm works at all.

**Why cold is second and not first:** it is the strongest proof we will ever have — *we found you with the thing we are selling you* — and it cannot run until the mailboxes are warm. The wait is not lost time; it forces the highest-converting channel first.

**Every week:** ~~one newsletter~~ *(R29 — parked)* · one DROP Show · one look at the number below. ⛓️ *Cadence of record is R31 (12 Aug): two posts a week + one monthly stats post.*

### ⭐ The one metric — accepted leads per client per month

> **20 is the floor. 200 is the business.**

20 is not a habit, it is the gate — `MIN_BATCH_APPROVALS = 20` in the code. A client who sits there drifts, hits the 30-day cold check (`COLD_DAYS = 30`) and suspends.

⚠️ **Never report client count without this number beside it.** Ten shallow clients and one deep client are the same revenue and a tenth of the profit.

**So the single most important job after someone pays is building the approval habit in week one.** Not onboarding. Not training. Habit.

## 5 · THE MONEY — four boxes

*Every figure below is computed from [`../CASHFLOW-LAB.html`](../CASHFLOW-LAB.html), the money model of record (PR6, founder-locked 25 Jul), at its own defaults.*

| Box | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | approvals × **$4** |
| **Cost to deliver** | approvals × **$0.83** *(sourcing $0.56 + working it $0.07 + card & FX $0.20)* + **$8/mo** for each client's inbox. Sourcing bills **at least $98/mo** in any month it runs — the data tier |
| **Fixed** | **$352/mo** — $146 platform + $206 company. Same whether we have one client or ten |
| **Net** | Revenue − Deliver − Fixed |

**You keep $3.17 of every $4.** One client covers their own inbox at 3 approvals a month; everything past that pays down the $352 and then it is profit.

**The line the whole strategy hangs on:**

> **Taking ONE client from 20 → 200 approvals adds $571/mo. A brand-new client at the 20 floor adds $55/mo.**
> **Deepening is worth 10× acquiring.**

That is why customer success is the revenue function here, not a support function — and why the target is ten right clients, deep, rather than a hundred shallow ones.
