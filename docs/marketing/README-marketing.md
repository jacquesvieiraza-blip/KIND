## Two rules that can cost you weeks

Repeated here because they are the expensive ones and they live in a checklist you'll read once.

1. ⛔ **Never authenticate `kindoutreach.com` or `trykind.org` in beehiiv.** Those are the four warming mailboxes, warm since 4 Aug, done ~25 Aug. One newsletter blast burns them and resets send-day.
2. ⛔ **Never change `gettingkind.com` MX.** That is Resend inbound reply-capture — the path proved end-to-end in A18. It breaks **silently**: replies just stop arriving.

✅ **Use `news.get-kind.com`, or beehiiv's own sending domain.**
