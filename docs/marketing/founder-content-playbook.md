## 1 · Your point of view

Content without a POV is a feature list. **The POV is the thing people follow.**

Fill this in once. Everything else derives from it.

> **Most people believe** [THE COMMON WISDOM IN YOUR NICHE]
> **I think that's wrong because** [WHAT YOU'VE SEEN]
> **What I actually believe is** [YOUR POSITION]
> **And the proof is** [WHAT YOU'VE BUILT / SEEN / MEASURED]

**A worked example from your own playbook** — Section 04 already contains it:

> **Most people believe** you need more outreach tools and more SDRs.
> **I think that's wrong because** the bottleneck isn't sending — it's deciding *who* is worth sending to, and that's the part everyone automates last.
> **What I actually believe is** AI should do the chasing; humans should do the closing.
> **The proof is** a system that researches and qualifies before a single email goes out.

**Tests for a real POV:**
- ✅ Can a reasonable person disagree with it? *(If not, it's a platitude.)*
- ✅ Does it follow from something you've actually seen? *(If not, it's a slogan.)*
- ✅ Would you say it out loud to a room? *(If not, cut the jargon until you would.)*

## 2 · The four pillars

Every post is one of these. If it isn't, don't post it.

| # | Pillar | What it is | Mix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Customer problems & real stories** | The pain, named precisely. A conversation you had | **40%** | The only pillar that makes strangers feel *seen*. Most founders under-do it |
| **2** | **Product in action** | The machine's actual decisions — what it picked and **why** | **25%** | ⭐ **Your unfair advantage.** Nobody else can post your system's reasoning |
| **3** | **Frameworks & tips** | Something they can use whether or not they buy | **20%** | Earns the follow. Gets saved and shared |
| **4** | **Founder journey & beliefs** | What you're building, what broke, what you learned | **15%** | Builds trust. **Least effective when it's the majority** — that's a diary, not marketing |

⚠️ **Most technical founders invert this** — 80% journey, 20% everything else. It feels authentic and it doesn't work, because your journey is interesting to you and useful to nobody until they already care.

## 3 · THE CONTENT BANK — do this today

**This runs regardless of R2 and it is the highest-value thing on this page.**

A plain text file. Every day, one line. That's the whole system.

```
2026-08-11  OBJECTION   "how is this different from Apollo" — third time this week
2026-08-11  REJECTION   rejected a 200-person company; said "too big, we lose to procurement"
2026-08-12  QUESTION    "what happens if the lead doesn't reply" — nobody has asked this before
2026-08-12  WIN         approved 9 of 10 on the first batch, said "these are actually right"
2026-08-13  FRICTION    watched onboarding — couldn't find the approve button for 40 seconds
```

**What to bank, every single day:**

| Tag | What | Why it's gold |
|---|---|---|
| `OBJECTION` | Exactly what they said, verbatim | Objections heard 3× are your next 3 posts **and** a copy change |
| `REJECTION` | A prospect rejected + **the reason** | Pillar 2. Nobody else has this data |
| `QUESTION` | Anything a human asked | If one asked, a hundred wondered |
| `WIN` | Something that worked, with the number | Proof, and you will forget it by Friday |
| `FRICTION` | Where someone got stuck | Fixes the product *and* writes the post |

**Two rules:** write it **verbatim** (your paraphrase drifts within a day), and write it **same day** (a memory of an objection is not an objection).

⚠️ **This is the same discipline as the register.** Things that live only in your head get contradicted; things written down the same session don't. It's true for founder rulings and it's true for customer objections.

## 4 · Turning one insight into a week

One good insight from the bank → **five outputs.** This is how you post five times a week without five ideas.

```
ONE BANKED INSIGHT
   e.g. "third person this week asked how we're different from Apollo"
   │
   ├─► LONG POST      the full answer, 200-300 words, with the real reason
   ├─► SHORT POST     just the sharpest sentence from it
   ├─► QUESTION POST  "what do you actually compare us to?" — invites replies
   ├─► NEWSLETTER     the long version + what you changed because of it
   └─► SALES SCRIPT   the objection-handler, now written down and reusable
```

**The rule: never invent a topic.** If you're staring at a blank page, the bank is empty — go and have a conversation. That is the *actual* fix, and it's why §3 outranks everything else here.

## 5 · The weekly rhythm 🔒

*Ready to switch on. Slots into the existing operating rhythm — see the rhythm artifact.*

| Day | Action | Time | Where it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Pull 5 insights from the bank. Pick 3 | 15 min | Sales block |
| **Tue** | Post 1 — problem/story (pillar 1) | 20 min | Sales block |
| **Wed** | Post 2 — product in action (pillar 2) | 20 min | Sales block |
| **Thu** | **Newsletter goes out.** Post 3 — the framework (pillar 3) | 45 min | Sales block |
| **Fri** | Post 4 — journey/belief (pillar 4). Reply to every comment | 20 min | Sales block |
| **Sat** | Write next week's issue from the bank | 60 min | Saturday block |
| **Sun** | **Off.** | — | Non-negotiable |

⚠️ **Replying to comments matters more than posting.** A reply is a conversation; a post is a broadcast. **Conversations are what the scoreboard counts.**

## 6 · Ten post ideas, tailored to you

Written for a founder with no audience — every one works at 40 followers.

| # | Idea | Pillar | Why it works with no audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Would you approve this prospect?"** — a real (anonymised) company, the fit reasoning, ask people to vote | 2 | Interactive, needs no reach, and every comment is a conversation |
| 2 | **"Three objections I heard this week, and my honest answers"** | 1 | Straight from the bank. Prospects recognise themselves |
| 3 | **"Why we rejected a company that looked perfect"** | 2 | Counter-intuitive, demonstrates judgement, nobody can copy it |
| 4 | **"The thing everyone gets wrong about outbound"** — your POV, stated plainly | 4 | Disagreement drives reach more reliably than agreement |
| 5 | **"I watched someone use it for the first time. Here's what broke."** | 4 | Founders trust founders who admit friction |
| 6 | **"How to tell if a prospect is worth chasing — 5 questions"** | 3 | Useful whether or not they buy. Gets saved |
| 7 | **"What $4 a lead actually buys"** — open-book unit economics | 3 | Transparency is rare and disproportionately memorable |
| 8 | **"The 30 days I spent building something nobody had asked for"** | 4 | Honest, and it's true |
| 9 | **"Referral-dependent pipeline: why it feels safe and isn't"** | 1 | Speaks directly to UK agency founders — your exact ICP |

**Idea 1 is the best one.** It costs nothing, needs no audience, produces comments (which is reach), and every single comment is a warm lead who has just told you how they think about fit.

## 7 · The weekly newsletter template

```
SUBJECT     specific, under 50 chars, no clickbait
            ✅ "Why we rejected a perfect-looking company"
            ❌ "You won't BELIEVE what happened this week"

HOOK        1-2 sentences. What happened, or the problem.

THE MEAT    200-350 words. ONE idea.
            A real example beats an opinion every time.

THE TAKE    2-3 lines. What you'd actually do about it.

CTA         ONE line. Usually: "hit reply and tell me X"

SIGN-OFF    — Jacques
```

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **300–500 words total** | Morning Brew's whole model. If it takes >3 min, cut |
| **One idea per issue** | Two ideas means neither lands |
| **One CTA** | Two CTAs is zero CTAs |
| **Plain text look** | Designed emails read as marketing; plain reads as a person |
| **Same day, every week** | ⭐ Consistency beats quality. A weak issue on time > a great one late |
| **Reply, don't click** | A reply starts a conversation. A click starts nothing |

## 8 · What not to do

| ❌ Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Post motivational filler | Everyone scrolls past it, including you |
| Use every post as a hard sell | Nobody follows a salesperson |
| Copy a trend with no relevance | Reach without fit is worse than no reach |
| Announce features nobody asked for | Post the *problem*, not the release note |
| Post the free-10 publicly | **R1** — it is your sales tool, not a public offer |
| Build a 3-month calendar up front | *"before you know which ideas earn attention"* — your own avoid-list |
| Post under your own name | **R2** — gated. This is the whole point of the banner |
