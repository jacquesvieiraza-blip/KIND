---

## 1 · The prerequisites — all six, no exceptions

Paid ads **buy more of a message that already works.** They do not find the message. Spending before you have one is paying to discover faster that you don't have one.

| ☐ | Prerequisite | Why it is non-negotiable |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **R7 lifted** — revenue exists, and ad spend is money you can lose entirely | It is not a test if losing it hurts |
| ☐ | **R2 lifted** | Ads are public |
| ☐ | **At least 3 paying clients** | Below that you don't know who your buyer is — you know who your first three friends' networks were |
| ☐ | **A message proven organically** | A post or a warm script that reliably produced replies. **The ad is that message, paid for** |
| ☐ | **The landing page converts** ≥20% of visitors to subscribers | Ads into a leaky page burn money at a fixed rate |
| ☐ | **Conversion tracking installed and verified with a real test** | ⭐ Without this you are guessing, and you will optimise toward the wrong thing |

⚠️ **A11 note:** if paid traffic arrives while 281 items remain unwalked, you are paying to send strangers into untested software. **Walk the money journeys first.**

## 2 · Which channel first

**Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — not Google.**

| | Meta | Google Search | YouTube / Demand Gen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Needs existing search demand? | ❌ no | ✅ **yes** | ❌ no |
| Good for an unknown category? | ✅ yes | ❌ no | ✅ yes |
| Cheap to test? | ✅ yes | ❌ expensive B2B keywords | ⚠️ needs video |
| Retargeting quality | ✅ best | ⚠️ ok | ✅ good |
| **Verdict** | ⭐ **START HERE** | **Skip for now** | **Phase 3** |

**The reason is simple and decides it:** Google Search captures people *already looking* for what you sell. **Nobody is searching for "AI that researches my prospects"** — the category is not established in their heads. They are searching "lead generation agency", where you'd bid against funded competitors for expensive clicks.

**Meta lets you interrupt the right person with the right problem** — *"still chasing pipeline between client work?"* — which is how you sell something people don't know exists.

**YouTube/Demand Gen is phase 3**, after Meta proves the message and you have video worth running.

## 3 · Retargeting FIRST — always

**Your first pound goes to people who already visited and didn't subscribe.** Never cold traffic.

| Why | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cheapest clicks you will ever buy** | They already know who you are |
| **Highest conversion** | They showed intent once |
| **Smallest budget needed** | The audience is tiny by definition |
| **Fastest honest signal** | If warm traffic won't convert, cold certainly won't |

⚠️ **You need traffic before you can retarget it.** This is why organic runs first — it *builds the audience the ads retarget.* Retargeting with 30 visitors a month does nothing.

**Audiences by funnel stage** *(straight from your playbook §18)*:

| Stage | Audience | The message |
|---|---|---|
| Visited, didn't subscribe | Page visitors, 30 days | The newsletter, restated |
| Subscribed, never opened | Custom list | One thing they missed |
| Opened, never replied | Custom list | A question that invites a reply |
| Replied, stalled | Custom list | Proof + the specific objection |

⛔ **Suppress converted users from every acquisition audience.** Paying to advertise the newsletter to existing subscribers is pure waste and it happens constantly.

## 4 · The 4-week test plan

**Budget ranges, not prescriptions.** Set them from what you can lose, not from what you hope to earn.

| Week | Spend/day | What you're testing | Kill / keep rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | £5–10 | **Retargeting only.** One audience, one creative | Does warm traffic convert at all? |
| **2** | £10–15 | **3 creative angles**, same audience, same landing page | Kill any with CTR <0.5% after 1,000 impressions |
| **3** | £15–25 | Winner from wk2 → **cold audience**, small | Kill if cost-per-subscriber >3× retargeting |
| **4** | £25–40 | Scale the winner **only** if week 3's economics held | **Stop entirely** if no qualified conversation came from paid |

**Total 4-week test: roughly £400–650.** Treat it as **spent, not invested.** If that number makes you uncomfortable, the gate is not open yet — and that is useful information, not a failure.

**The three creative angles to test** *(your playbook §17)*:

1. **Referral dependence** — *"Your pipeline is one relationship away from a bad quarter."*
2. **Senior time cost** — *"Your best people are prospecting instead of delivering."*
3. **Anti-hype** — *"You don't need another email tool. You need pipeline."* ⭐ Usually the winner in a crowded category

## 5 · Campaign structures

### Meta

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | **Conversions** → subscribe event. *(Never "Traffic" — you'll get cheap clicks from people who never convert)* |
| **Audience — retargeting** | Page visitors 30d · video viewers 50%+ · page engagers |
| **Audience — cold** | UK · agency/consultancy job titles · founder/director/MD · 5–30 employee company size |
| **Placement** | Automatic to start. Narrow only once data says so |
| **Creative** | 3 angles × static image or 15s founder video |
| **Copy** | Problem first line · one specific claim · one CTA |
| **CTA button** | **Subscribe** or **Learn More.** Never "Sign Up" — it implies an account |
| **Landing** | The beehiiv page. **Never the homepage** |

⚠️ **Meta needs ~50 conversions/week per ad set to optimise properly.** At small budgets you won't hit that, so **judge on cost-per-subscriber, not on Meta's own optimisation** — it is guessing at your volume too.

### YouTube / Google Demand Gen *(phase 3)*

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Leads → subscribe conversion |
| **Targeting** | Custom segments by *intent*, plus your retargeting lists |
| **Creative** | 30–60s founder-to-camera. **The first 5 seconds are the whole ad** |
| **CTA** | One line, on screen and spoken |
| **Landing** | Same beehiiv page. **One page, always** |

**Do not start here.** It needs video production and a proven message. It is the third channel, not the first.

## 6 · What to track, and what to ignore

| ✅ Track | ❌ Ignore |
|---|---|
| Cost per subscriber | Impressions |
| Subscriber → reply rate | Reach |
| **Replies → conversations** | Likes and shares |
| **Conversations → clients** | CTR *on its own* |
| Cost per **conversation** | "Engagement rate" |
| Cost per **client** | Cost per click |

**The only number that matters is cost per client.** Everything above it is a step on the way and can look healthy while the bottom line doesn't move.

⚠️ **The classic trap:** cheap clicks, growing list, zero conversations. It feels like it's working for about six weeks. **Watch replies, not signups.**

---

## 7 · Kill rules — decide these now, before money is on the table

Written in advance because in the moment you will want to give a losing ad "one more week."

| Rule | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Kill a creative** | CTR <0.5% after 1,000 impressions |
| **Kill an audience** | 0 conversions after 3× your target cost-per-subscriber |
| **Kill the whole test** | 4 weeks, real spend, **zero qualified conversations** |
| **Increase budget** | Cost-per-subscriber stable for 5 straight days **and** subscribers are replying |
| **Double down** | An angle produces conversations at **half** the cost of the others |

**Scale by 20–30% at a time, never by doubling.** Big jumps reset the platform's learning and you lose the thing that was working.

## 8 · When this gate opens

**Not a date — a condition.** All of these, together:

- ✅ 3+ paying clients
- ✅ Revenue covers the cost floor (**$468/mo once live**)
- ✅ A11 money journeys walked
- ✅ An organic message that reliably produces replies
- ✅ Landing page converting ≥20%
- ✅ Ad budget is money you can lose without it mattering

**Until then this document sits here, finished, costing nothing.** That is its job.
