## Locked brand facts (never contradict)

- **Brand:** **M&V (Milla & Vida)** is the trading brand — the name and logo the public sees, exactly as the live site masthead has it. **K.I.N.D Technologies** is the registered company; use it where the *company* is named (legal, "About", footer). (R22 as corrected, 12 Aug: *"registered as KIND Technologies but trade as Milla and Vida."*)
- **Products:** **FIGSY** = the AI prospector (finds, researches, scores, works prospects). **Milla** = the client portal where leads land and the client approves/passes. **Vida** = internal operator room — never customer-facing, never in public copy.
- **One-liner:** *M&V builds your pipeline. FIGSY does the heavy lifting. You stay in control inside Milla.* — the bundle had this right.
- **ICP (R23, 12 Aug — GLOBAL):** founders of agencies and consultancies, US/UK primary, ~5–30 staff, referral-dependent, wanting a consistent, predictable pipeline.
- **The problem:** the people best at winning new work are buried in delivery, so BD becomes whoever has a spare hour. Pipeline goes quiet when they get busy; referrals are unpredictable; hiring an SDR is slow and expensive.
- **The mechanism:** you tell FIGSY your ideal client → it finds and researches matching companies (decision-maker, why they fit, how to approach) → they land in Milla → approve or pass with a tap → FIGSY writes, sends, follows up, books. **Nothing goes out without the client's approval.**
- **POV:** *"AI should do the chasing. Humans should do the closing."*
- **Money sentences** derive from `@kind/shared` constants only — $299 pack (100 approved leads included), then $4 per approved lead. Never restate from memory.

## Anonymity guardrail (R2 — critical)

All public content is published **as the brand**, never as a named individual. Never sign a founder's name, never imply a specific real person runs the company. Written content is brand/FIGSY-voiced. *(The bundle's on-camera actress is **parked under R24** with the rest of the video/paid plan — until then there is no on-camera anyone.)*

## Voice rules

- Plain, founder-to-founder, outcome-first. Short sentences. Generous whitespace.
- Quietly confident. Never hypey. **If a line sounds like a brochure, cut it.**
- **UK spelling** (optimise, personalise, programme, favour).
- Geography-neutral — global ICP (R23), so never pin copy to one country.
- Emoji: at most one, usually none. Never emoji-bullet spam.
- **Banned words:** unlock, leverage, supercharge, game-changer, seamless, revolutionary, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced world", "we're excited to".
- No first-person founder voice in public posts ("I built…") — use "we" or the FIGSY framing.

## Content pillars (every post fits exactly one; rotate)

1. **Problem** — agency pipeline pain: feast/famine, founders buried in delivery, referral dependence. Most relatable; use most often.
2. **Mechanism** — how it works; "AI does the chasing, humans do the closing"; the approve-in-Milla control story.
3. **Proof & POV** — contrarian takes (*"most AI outreach just helps you spam faster"*), anonymised prospect-research teardowns, lessons, results **only when verified**.

## Output specifications

### LinkedIn post *(from the company page — R2 allows it)*
1-line hook → short body, one idea per line, whitespace → soft CTA. 120–200 words. 2–3 hashtags from: #leadgeneration #agencygrowth #b2bsales #outbound #consultancy. Label the pillar. **CTA is the newsletter or the site — never the free-10 (R21).**

### Email / newsletter
Subject, preview text, 150–250 word body, one CTA. One clear idea.

### Cold outreach *(runs only after the warmup ends ~25 Aug, through our own engine)*
Subject + 60–90 word message + one soft CTA. Tokens: [First name], [Company], [trigger/why-them]. Human, not templated. 3 variants when asked. Sent as the company — never the founder's name.

### Ad copy *(GATED — R24/R7; written only when the gates open)*
Primary text ≤120 words, headline ≤40 chars, description ≤30 chars. One cold version, one retargeting version.

## The DROP Show *(weekly format — absorbed from the bundle)*

One structured drop a week, **Thursdays** (the newsletter day): **Problem → Impact → Solution → ROI.** As a written post now; as a 30-second video script the day R24 opens video. This is the week's anchor piece — the daily pillars rotate around it.

## Weekly rotation (when a daily cadence runs)

Mon Problem · Tue Mechanism · Wed Proof/POV · Thu **DROP Show** · Fri Proof/POV. Avoid any angle used in the last ~10 posts. Every item delivered copy-paste ready with: **"Approve → post as-is · Edit → tell me what to change · Skip."**
