# Warm Outreach Kit — the messages you send this week

> **Origin:** the Cowork bundle's traffic starter kit, absorbed 12 Aug and made lock-compliant. The **personal** messages keep the free-10 offer — that is **R1/R21 working as ruled** (*"the free offer is my call when i need it"*): you, offering it, to a named person. The **public posts** had free-10 CTAs and were re-voiced — a public post inviting anyone to claim is a public offer, which R21 vetoes. Their CTA now invites a conversation; you decide in the DM whether to offer the 10.
>
> **The engine:** build the warm-100 list → 5 personally-written messages a day (MARKETING-PLAN D1) → log every reply reason (D2) → bank every objection (D3). No message sent twice verbatim.

## Engine 1 · Warm messages — personal, one-to-one

*The free-10 appears here because these are personal offers from you. Adapt the brackets; never mass-paste.*

**Opener A — past client / someone you've worked with**
> Hi [Name] — hope you and the team are well. I've built something I reckon you'd find useful. It's called FIGSY: an AI prospector that finds and researches companies matching your ideal client, then hands you a shortlist to approve — so the outbound gets done without pulling you off delivery. I'm setting a few people up with their first 10 researched prospects, free. Want me to run yours? Two minutes once you tell me who you sell to.

**Opener B — founder peer / warm LinkedIn contact**
> Hey [Name] — pipeline's the eternal headache for agencies like yours, so this might land at a good time. I've been building an AI prospector (FIGSY) that does the chasing your senior people don't have time for — it finds the fit, researches the decision-maker, and you just approve who gets contacted. I'll research your first 10 prospects free so you can see the quality. Fancy giving it a go?

**Opener C — short DM**
> [Name] — quick one. Want me to run your first 10 researched prospects, free? I've built an AI prospector for agencies and I'm setting a handful up this week. All I need is who your ideal client is.

**Follow-up 1 — 3–4 days, no reply**
> No worries if the timing's off, [Name] — just bumping this in case it got buried. The offer stands whenever you fancy seeing what your pipeline could look like.

**Follow-up 2 — a week later, close the loop**
> Last nudge from me, [Name] — I'll leave you in peace. If it's not for you, no problem at all. And if anyone in your network is fighting the feast-or-famine pipeline thing, I'd love an intro.

**Referral ask — non-buyers and the wider network**
> Quick favour, [Name] — do you know any agency or consultancy founders who'd like a steadier pipeline? I'm setting a few of them up right now, and happy to do the same for anyone you point my way.

⚠️ **Discipline that makes this compound:** record every reply **reason** (interested / not now / not a fit — verbatim, same day, into the content bank). Three of anything is next week's post and a copy change.

## Engine 2 · Content posts — public, brand-voiced *(company page — R2)*

*Re-voiced 12 Aug: brand voice per `voice.md` (no "I built…"), and the public free-10 CTA replaced per R21 — the post invites a conversation; the offer stays yours to make in the DM.*

**Post 1 · the problem**
> The best salesperson in most agencies is the founder.
> Who's also far too busy delivering to actually sell.
> So business development becomes whoever has a spare hour — which is no one. The pipeline goes quiet the moment things get busy, and three months later there's a dry spell nobody saw coming.
> Referrals paper over it. Until they don't.
> And hiring someone to prospect is slow, expensive — and now you're managing a hire instead of your clients.
> The problem was never being bad at winning work. It's that the person best at it has no time to do it.
> That's what FIGSY exists to fix — an AI prospector that does the chasing, so the humans can do the closing.
> If that's your pipeline, tell us who you sell to — we'll show you what it could look like.

**Post 2 · the mechanism**
> AI should do the chasing. Humans should do the closing.
> That's the whole idea.
> You say who your ideal client is. FIGSY finds companies that match, researches each one — who the decision-maker is, why they fit, how to open the conversation — and drops a scored shortlist in front of you.
> Approve or pass with a tap. Nothing goes out without your say-so.
> No SDR to manage. No cold list to wade through. Researched prospects, ready to review — and you stay in control of every one.
> Curious what that looks like for your agency? Send us who you sell to.

**Post 3 · the point of view**
> Most AI outreach tools just help you spam faster.
> More sends, more sequences, more "personalisation" that fools no one — into inboxes that stopped reading.
> We think that's backwards. The hard part of outbound was never the sending. It's knowing **who** is worth contacting and **why** — the research a good salesperson does before they ever write a line.
> So that's what the AI should do: the finding, the researching, the qualifying. The human stays where humans win — the judgement and the close.
> AI does the chasing. Humans do the closing.
