The one thing that decides the money

The demo writes to 6 people a week per client, and its team can check 45 people a day. The money plan (section 10) assumes about 1,000 people a month per client, with 1 held meeting per 500. They don't fit together:

  • At 6 a week and 1 in 500, a client gets one held meeting every 19 months. To earn 2 a month ($1,400) from 6 a week, about 1 person in 13 would have to end in a held meeting.
  • At 45 checks a day, 1,000 people a month needs about one full-time checker per client.
  • Section 10 counts about 30 seconds a check. If a full check of three emails and the research takes a minute, the plan case still makes about 65%, but your worst case falls to about 18%, below your 35% (R200 ⑧). At the worst case, a check has to take under about 40 seconds.

Recommendation: build it the same either way, with people per week as a setting for each client. Let the pilot measure the two numbers (H7): how many people it takes to get one held meeting, and how long one check takes. Set the weekly number from them at week 6 (D11), and make the quality queue as fast as an honest check allows (C5).
