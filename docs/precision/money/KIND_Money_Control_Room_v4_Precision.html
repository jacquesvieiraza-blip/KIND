K.I.N.D Money Control Room — v4 · precision · in $ (8 Oct)

Rebuilt for the precision model: each month we find the few companies with a real, dated reason to talk to the client now, research each person, write to each one individually, a person checks every message, and nothing is sent until the client approves. The client pays a one-off setup fee, then one price for each meeting that takes place. No meeting target, no cap per meeting, no 50/50, no wallet credit.
Planning rules for this model: one price for every client ("we cant charge different values", 7 Oct) · the client pays for setup ("client pays for set up", 7 Oct) · at least 35% margin at the worst case ("i need to make at least 35% on worst case", 7 Oct), and the worst case is 1 held meeting per 1,300 people (7 Oct) · US prospects first, so prices are in $ (R201 ⑥, 8 Oct); the company is UK-registered, so the UK tax and cash lines stay in £ · Apollo is the only data provider (R146, 23 Sep) · partners frozen (R139, 23 Sep), maths kept · Coaching is coming soon, nothing in Coaching approved to build (R188, corrected 2 Oct); your 2 Oct price was $100 a meeting (R188 Q11).
The decided model (8 Oct, R200–R202). $1,500 setup and $700 per held meeting, live on the website since 8 Oct; 2 mailboxes per client at 50 a day in the client's own Google account; 100 people per client a week to start. Milla's own billing still takes the old programme payment until card billing (M4) ships. Every money figure on this page is shown in $ at the £1 = $ rate set below. Numbers marked UNKNOWN aren't measured yet: Apollo's price for extra credits (D9), credits used per person, and the AI cost per person (pilot row H7). Every number is editable.
What changed from v3: the Founders / Growth / Enterprise bands → one price · "programme" with a meeting target, a people cap, 50/50 or full upfront and wallet credit → setup fee + per held meeting, billed after each meeting happens · 250 / 300 / 400 people per meeting → 1 held meeting per N people (plan 1 in 500, worst 1 in 1,300) · sending mailboxes: the client can pay Google directly (decision D8) · Smartlead and ChatGPT flagged as not needed · PDL removed (retired, R146) · Pool reuse removed (old volume code) · target 70% → 35% at the worst case · Coaching added as coming soon · company floor and tax unchanged · 8 Oct, later: prices in $, $1,500 setup and $700 per held meeting (R201 ⑥); results shown in $, UK tax and cash in £.
