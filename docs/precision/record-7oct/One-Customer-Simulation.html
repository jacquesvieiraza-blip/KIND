5 · What decides stop or continue, cheaply

None of this tells you the hit rate, and that is the only number that matters. You can measure it for almost nothing:

  • Get House sending again and put 1,000 people through all 5 emails. That uses the 4 mailboxes and Apollo plan you already pay for.
  • Read the result: if 1 in 250 is true, 0 meetings from 1,000 people has only a 2% chance. So 0 meetings means stop: the message, the targeting or the inboxing is wrong, and more tech won't fix it. 3 or more means the plan rate is real. Then decide on pricing (drop $99), the mailbox pool, and your sending limits.
  • Time: about 10 weeks at today's 100 a day for House, or about 5 weeks if you raise House's daily limit to 200.
