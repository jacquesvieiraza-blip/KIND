Website wording map

Every live page of get-kind.com, element by element: what it says now and what it should say under the precision model. Read from the live code (version ef83a99). Nothing has been changed.

The brief this follows

  • Layout, UI, colours, images and sections all stay. Only the words change. Every element keeps its place and order.
  • The colour sweep on the big headlines stays automatically. It belongs to the headline, not to particular words, so new wording gets the same effect. New headlines are kept close to the current length; any much longer one is flagged.
  • [Square brackets] are decisions you haven't made: the setup fee and the price per held meeting (D1), what counts as held (D2), the emails being in the client's name from their own mailboxes (D8 / T5), the data sources, and pilot results (H5). They're filled in only after you decide.
  • Coaching stays (your update, 7 Oct). On Pricing, the Coaching rows remain as the optional paid layer, marked "Coming soon" with no price until it launches (R188 Q13, Q14). Everyone gets the meeting card and "How did it go?"; paid Coaching is offered after the 3rd held meeting.
  • No invented numbers. No promised meeting counts, reply rates or ROI multiples. Legitimate interest, never consent. No South Africa. US first.
