The names behind the names

Named after the people who matter most.

These aren't product names invented in a boardroom. They're the founder's family — his mother, his father, and his two daughters — and their personalities are the product.

The Opener

FIGSY

That's me — the founder, the father, and the son. The Opener. Twenty years in sales taught me it was never really about selling; it's about listening deeply, understanding people, and helping them move from a problem to a better place. I started at the bottom, took the knocks, and kept showing up. My drive doesn't only come from ambition — it comes from love, and from the joy of a real conversation where someone finally feels heard. My ikigai is simple: solving problems. That's the FIGSY agent — it opens doors others don't see, turning conversations into solutions, and solutions into opportunity.

The Brain

Milla

My eldest daughter. Razor-sharp — she'll catch the flaw in your logic before you've finished the sentence, and she's usually three steps ahead. The critical thinker. Before building the product, I asked myself: what would someone who thinks like Milla do with your data? That's the Milla agent (coming soon) — reading your business, questioning the obvious, and surfacing intelligence before you know you need it.

The Connector

Vida

My youngest daughter. Always talking, always smiling cheek to cheek — she lights up every room she walks into and makes everyone feel like they belong. The chatterbox who connects. Vida the agent (coming soon) will have that same energy: on your website, on WhatsApp, 24/7 — never missing a conversation, never letting a warm lead go cold.

The Closer

Denise

My mother. She built a successful sales business from nothing — and closed not by pushing, but because people genuinely liked and trusted her. The personality everyone remembered long after the meeting ended. The relationship closer. That's the Denise agent (coming soon): warm, confident, zero desperation — she picks up where FIGSY leaves off and turns a booked meeting into a signed deal.

The Order Maker

Tony

My dad. The man people rely on when things get messy — he doesn't chase attention, he watches, finds the pattern, and quietly brings everything into line. Honest in the way he speaks, works, and treats people. The Order Maker. That's the Tony agent: the steady hand behind the scenes that keeps operations running — making sure promises are kept, standards are met, and everyone knows what happens next. He brings order where others only see chaos.
