Alta AI GTM / autonomous SDR platform 50+ data sources; CRM, intent, job postings, news and product usage; signal-based timing; email, LinkedIn, SMS, WhatsApp and calls; condition-based branching; Closed Lost revival; AI agents that book meetings. Direct benchmark for end-to-end autonomous outbound and CRM activation.
Regie.ai AI sales engagement / agentic prospecting 100+ built-in signals; first-, second- and third-party signals; sources such as Google, LinkedIn, G2, Crunchbase, BuiltWith, filings, news, call transcripts and CRM records; static/dynamic sequencing; custom enrichment waterfalls. Benchmark for wide signal ingestion and AI-orchestrated prospecting.
Unify Signal-led outbound orchestration Signals from website visits, product usage, job changes and other events; plays can trigger enrichment, prospecting, AI agents, sequencing and alerts; signal-triggered outbound workflows. Strong benchmark for Get-Kind's proposed WHY-NOW / signal-to-campaign engine.
Amplemarket Signal-based sales intelligence and outreach Millions of signals; community / Slack activity, job changes and custom intent; contextual prospect insights; human-in-the-loop AI assistance; social-selling workflows. Relevant benchmark for social intent and human-controlled AI selling.
Apollo B2B data + sales engagement platform Large contact/account database; verified contact data; enrichment; lead scoring/signals; sequences; CRM sync; meetings; AI/MCP access for prospecting, enrichment and sequence actions. Major data/engagement incumbent and an existing Get-Kind data-source relationship.
Clay GTM data and enrichment infrastructure 200+ enrichments/providers; any enrichment can become a signal; technology changes, job changes, news, product data and social/content monitoring; audiences unify CRM, warehouse, signals and enrichment. Best benchmark for a future Lead Evidence Graph and intelligent enrichment waterfall.
Common Room Buyer intelligence / person-level signal resolution 50+ native signal integrations plus agentic capture; social, community, product, web and other signals; Person360 identity resolution; waterfall enrichment; signals unified to person/account profiles. Best benchmark for the proposed Social Intent + identity-resolution layer.
Outreach Enterprise sales engagement Mature multi-step sequences, triggers, rulesets, schedules, email, voice, SMS, calendar/meetings and AI agents; detailed sequence execution and attribution. Benchmark for sequencing maturity, governance and meeting attribution.
6sense Enterprise account intent / ABM intelligence Account/contact intent including keywords, web activity, technographics, psychographics and job postings. Benchmark for high-end intent intelligence rather than a direct reseller meeting engine.
