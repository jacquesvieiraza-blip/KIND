Get-Kind — Jack & Jill Inspired Product Model Verification

Purpose: read-only Claude Code verification of whether the current Get-Kind architecture can support a more differentiated product model built around Milla, Vida, FIGSY, a living Meeting Brief, calibration, signals/anti-signals and learning toward MEETING_BOOKED.

This is not a request to copy Jack & Jill's recruitment product. It is a request to test whether the same product-design principles can be expressed coherently in Get-Kind's outbound-reseller model.

2. Product roles to verify

MILLA — Client Intelligence Layer

Target role: deeply understand the client's business, commercial objective, ICP preferences, exclusions, campaign feedback and meeting goals.

Not merely a portal/dashboard.

FIGSY — Decision Brain

Target role: decide who to pursue, why now, which evidence matters, which campaign angle fits, whether the prospect is eligible, and what should happen next.

Not merely a 0–100 lead scorer.

VIDA — Human/Operator Control Layer

Target role: handle exceptions, uncertainty, risk, approvals, anomalies and cases requiring judgement.

Not routine manual administration.

Claude must identify the real production responsibilities currently assigned to Milla, Vida and FIGSY, if those names/concepts exist in code or product structure.

7. Signals and anti-signals

Get-Kind should be able to learn both why a prospect is attractive and why a prospect should be avoided.

Signals

  • new sales leader;
  • funding;
  • SDR hiring;
  • technology change;
  • social intent;
  • relevant public activity;
  • client-defined commercial triggers.

Anti-signals

  • company too small/large;
  • wrong business model;
  • recent rejection;
  • cool-down active;
  • no relevant team/function;
  • company contraction / unsuitable timing;
  • client-defined permanent exclusions.

Claude should verify whether anti-signals are first-class structured data or are currently represented only through ad-hoc filtering/suppression.

8. State and recency

A prospect should not be treated as a static profile.

ICP FIT      85
INTENT       91
RECENCY      98
TIMING       94

WHY NOW:
New VP Sales
+ hiring 6 SDRs
+ relevant public signal 3 days ago

Claude should inspect whether the current architecture can represent:

  • signal observed time;
  • signal age / decay;
  • current vs historical state;
  • expired/stale evidence;
  • recency-aware scoring;
  • time-dependent campaign eligibility.

16. What NOT to copy from Jack & Jill

Claude should not propose a two-sided candidate/employer marketplace or a “prospect agent” simply because that pattern exists in recruitment.

Get-Kind's market is structurally different:

Recruitment:
candidate wants opportunity
company wants candidate

Outbound:
client wants prospect
prospect may not yet want conversation

The transferable principle is therefore better understanding + timing + relevance + low-friction progression, not a literal mutual-agent marketplace.
