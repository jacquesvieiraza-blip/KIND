Get-Kind — Meeting Booking Engine Strategy

Commercial objective: maximise ICP-valid meetings booked for clients. The optimisation boundary stops at meeting_booked. Client/reseller conversion after the meeting is outside this strategy.

1. The North Star

Primary conversion event: meeting_booked

Get-Kind should optimise sourcing, campaign selection, sequencing, messaging, reply handling and booking flow around one outcome: more ICP-valid meetings booked per client.

3. Sell the response before the meeting

Cold outreach should often seek a small expression of interest before asking for calendar time.

EMAIL 1
→ relevance
→ problem
→ reason now
→ low-friction interest CTA

Examples:
"Is this something you're looking at?"
"Would this be relevant?"
"Worth me sending over how we're doing this?"
"Is reducing X currently a priority?"

specific available times
      +
booking link
      ↓
MEETING BOOKED

4. Every sequence step should earn its place

A sequence should not repeat the same pitch in different words.

EMAIL 1
Why you + why now

EMAIL 2
Different commercial problem

EMAIL 3
Relevant proof / result / evidence

EMAIL 4
Alternative angle or objection

EMAIL 5
Low-friction final question
Five emails should create five different opportunities to respond.

A sequence that only says “following up”, “bumping this” and “did you see this?” is effectively one attempt repeated several times.

5. FIGSY should choose the angle, not just score the lead

Instead of only returning a score, FIGSY should eventually reason about the commercial context behind the prospect.

WHY THIS COMPANY?
→ hiring 8 SDRs

WHY THIS PERSON?
→ VP Sales

WHY NOW?
→ started role 31 days ago

LIKELY PROBLEM?
→ rapidly building outbound capacity

BEST CAMPAIGN ANGLE?
→ pipeline generation without increasing SDR headcount

BEST PROOF?
→ relevant customer scenario

BEST OPENING?
→ hiring-growth angle

This creates far narrower, more relevant campaigns than a generic “VP Sales campaign”.

8. Positive reply → booked meeting is its own conversion engine

A key KPI should be:

positive_reply_to_booking_rate
=
booked_meetings / qualified_positive_replies

The system should be able to identify where interested prospects are lost:

  • positive reply received but not reviewed quickly;
  • human approval waiting too long;
  • reply does not answer the prospect's actual question;
  • no concrete times are offered;
  • booking link is omitted;
  • timezone or availability creates friction;
  • booking flow errors.
Positive reply

14. Metric hierarchy

  1. ICP-valid meetings booked
  2. Meetings booked per 100 eligible prospects
  3. Positive replies per 100 delivered
  4. Positive reply → meeting booked %
  5. Data cost per meeting booked
Stop measurement at meeting booked for this product optimisation model.

No meeting attendance, opportunity creation, close rate, reseller conversion or revenue attribution is required here. Those belong to the reseller/client after handoff.
