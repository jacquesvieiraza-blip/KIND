3. Role simplification hypothesis

EntityInternal roleRecommended public visibility
Milla Client-facing intelligence and relationship layer. Primary public character / product interface.
FIGSY Decision engine: who, why now, campaign angle, scoring, eligibility, next-best action. Secondary. Explain later as the intelligence behind Milla.
Vida Operator / operations layer, increasingly autonomous over time. Mostly hidden from acquisition messaging unless operational trust matters.
Nexus Isolation / learning / platform architecture. Trust / architecture section only, not early hero messaging.

Claude should verify whether the current website forces a first-time visitor to understand too many internal product names before understanding the outcome.

4. Homepage comprehension test

Claude should inspect the current homepage and answer:

  1. Can a new buyer explain what Get-Kind does after reading only the hero?
  2. Does the hero communicate MEETING_BOOKED, or primarily “managed leads” / “AI outbound” / internal system structure?
  3. How many proprietary names or concepts are introduced in the first screen?
  4. Does the page explain Milla, Vida, FIGSY and Nexus before proving why the buyer should care?
  5. Is the primary CTA low friction and obvious?
  6. Does the page sell software mechanics or client outcomes?
  7. Does the visitor understand who they interact with day-to-day?
