🎯 Design principle (locked) — the portal stays a narrow revenue surface

Generalists (Monday, ClickUp, Notion) sprawl into 10-tile app grids. That breadth is their trap, not our target. If K.I.N.D ever ships a "More" grid it is ≤5 tiles, all revenue. Every new surface must answer: "does this directly help the client get a meeting / close?"
